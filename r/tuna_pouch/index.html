Tuna Pouch

Description

A ready-to-eat pouch of tuna — one of the leanest, highest protein-per-calorie snacks there is. No draining, no prep.

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 1
  • Cuisine: snack
  • Primary Protein: fish
  • Calories: 110 per serving
  • Protein: 25g
  • Fat: 1g

Ingredients

  • 95 g tuna (One pouch. Flavoured pouches need no extras.)

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Tear open a tuna pouch and eat straight from it — flavoured pouches need nothing added.
