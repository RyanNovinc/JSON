Egg Muffins

Description

A batch of 12 cottage-cheese-blended egg muffins with sausage and spinach. One serving is 3 muffins, served with avo toast.

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 4
  • Cuisine: breakfast
  • Primary Protein: eggs
  • Calories: 790 per serving
  • Protein: 44g
  • Carbs: 63g
  • Fat: 41g

Ingredients

  • 8 count egg whole
  • 250 g cottage cheese (Blended into the eggs.)
  • 200 g breakfast sausage (Browned and crumbled.)
  • 80 g cheese tasty grated
  • 60 g spinach baby (Chopped.)
  • 60 g brown onion (Diced.)
  • 5 g olive oil (For greasing the tin.)
  • 0.5 tsp salt
  • 0.5 tsp black pepper ground

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Preheat the oven to 180C (350F) and grease a 12-cup muffin tin. If the sausage is raw, brown and crumble it; chop the spinach and onion.
  2. Step 2: Blend the eggs, cottage cheese, salt, and pepper until smooth.
  3. Step 3: Divide the sausage, spinach, onion, and half the cheese between the cups, pour the egg mixture over to fill each 3/4 full, and top with the rest of the cheese.
  4. Step 4: Bake for 18-22 minutes until set and puffy, then cool for 5 minutes and remove.
