Protein Shake

Description

The fastest protein hit going — a scoop of protein powder shaken or blended with milk. The classic post-workout grab.

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 1
  • Cuisine: snack
  • Primary Protein: dairy
  • Calories: 250 per serving
  • Protein: 35g
  • Carbs: 16g
  • Fat: 5g

Ingredients

  • 35 g whey protein vanilla (About 1-1.5 scoops.)
  • 350 ml full cream milk (Milk for more calories; water for a leaner shake.)

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Add the protein powder and milk to a shaker, shake until smooth, and drink.
