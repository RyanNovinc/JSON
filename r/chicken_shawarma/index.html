Chicken Shawarma

Description

Marinated chicken with salad and yogurt sauce in a large tortilla

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 4
  • Cuisine: mediterranean
  • Primary Protein: chicken
  • Calories: 738 per serving
  • Protein: 51g
  • Carbs: 62g
  • Fat: 31g

Ingredients

  • 800 g chicken thigh skinless
  • 200 g greek yoghurt plain
  • 5 cloves garlic clove
  • 28 g olive oil
  • 1 count lemon
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 tsp ground coriander
  • 2 tsp paprika sweet
  • 1 tsp ground turmeric
  • 200 g cherry tomatoes
  • 200 g cucumber
  • 150 g brown onion
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 0.5 tsp black pepper ground

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Make marinade with yogurt, garlic, lemon juice, spices, oil, salt and pepper
  2. Step 2: Marinate chicken for at least 30 minutes
  3. Step 3: Cook chicken in hot pan until done, rest and slice
  4. Step 4: Prepare vegetables - dice tomatoes, cucumber, slice onion
  5. Step 5: Assemble wraps or bowls with chicken, vegetables and remaining yogurt sauce
