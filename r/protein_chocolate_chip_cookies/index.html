Protein Chocolate Chip Cookies

Description

Soft-baked flourless chocolate chip cookies — peanut butter, protein powder, and egg form the dough, no flour. Makes 10.

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 10
  • Cuisine: dessert
  • Primary Protein: dairy
  • Calories: 210 per serving
  • Protein: 11g
  • Carbs: 14g
  • Fat: 14g

Ingredients

  • 200 g peanut butter natural (Smooth. Forms the flourless dough base.)
  • 50 g whey protein vanilla (Vanilla or chocolate. Whey blends/casein bake softer than isolate.)
  • 2 count egg whole
  • 60 g honey (Or coconut sugar for a more classic cookie spread.)
  • 0.5 tsp baking powder (Or 1/2 tsp baking soda.)
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 80 g chocolate chips (Save some to press on top.)
  • 0.25 tsp salt

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Preheat the oven to 175°C (350°F) and line a baking tray with baking paper.
  2. Step 2: Mix the peanut butter, eggs, honey, and vanilla into a smooth paste.
  3. Step 3: Stir in the protein powder, baking powder, and salt to form a soft dough, then fold in most of the chocolate chips.
  4. Step 4: Scoop 10 mounds onto the tray, flatten slightly, and press the remaining chips on top.
  5. Step 5: Bake 8-10 minutes until the edges are golden, then cool on the tray to firm up.
