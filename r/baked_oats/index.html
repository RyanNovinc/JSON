Baked Oats

Description

A cake-like baked oat pudding with whey in the batter, topped with Greek yoghurt and berries.

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 1
  • Cuisine: breakfast
  • Primary Protein: dairy
  • Calories: 800 per serving
  • Protein: 51g
  • Carbs: 104g
  • Fat: 19g

Ingredients

  • 80 g rolled oats raw
  • 30 g whey protein vanilla (1 scoop.)
  • 1 count egg whole (Binds the batter.)
  • 120 ml full cream milk
  • 60 g banana (Mashed into the batter.)
  • 21 g honey (Approximately 1 tbsp.)
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 0.5 tsp cinnamon ground

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Preheat the oven to 180C (350F) and mash the banana in a bowl.
  2. Step 2: Add the oats, whey, egg, milk, honey, baking powder, and cinnamon, and mix to a smooth batter.
  3. Step 3: Pour into a greased oven dish or large ramekin and bake for 20-22 minutes until set and golden on top.
