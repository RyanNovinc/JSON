Honey Chicken

Description

Chinese-takeaway honey chicken — cornstarch-coated pan-crisped chicken tossed in a glossy honey-soy glaze, over jasmine rice. Lighter pan-fried version for meal prep (deep-fried battered is the indulgent alternative). Makes 4.

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 4
  • Cuisine: asian
  • Primary Protein: chicken
  • Calories: 786 per serving
  • Protein: 54g
  • Carbs: 104g
  • Fat: 15g

Ingredients

  • 800 g chicken breast (Breast (leaner, fits the lighter version) or thigh. Bite-size. 200g per serving.)
  • 370 g jasmine rice (2 cups uncooked.)
  • 40 g cornstarch (Most to coat the chicken + a little for the glaze slurry.)
  • 28 g olive oil (Shallow-fry; most drains off (only ~20g retained in macros).)
  • 90 g honey (Glaze base — the main carb/sugar contributor; defining feature.)
  • 45 ml soy sauce
  • 30 ml rice vinegar
  • 10 ml sesame oil
  • 3 cloves garlic clove (Minced.)
  • 9 g sesame seeds (Garnish.)
  • 2 count spring onion (Garnish.)

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Rinse the jasmine rice until the water runs clear, then cook per packet and keep warm.
  2. Step 2: Toss the bite-size chicken in cornstarch with a pinch of salt to coat.
  3. Step 3: Shallow-fry / pan-crisp the chicken in oil over medium-high until golden and cooked through, then drain.
  4. Step 4: Make the glaze: simmer honey, soy, rice vinegar, sesame oil, garlic, and a cornstarch slurry until glossy and thickened.
  5. Step 5: Toss the crispy chicken through the glaze just before serving to keep it crisper.
  6. Step 6: Serve over rice, garnished with sesame seeds and spring onion.
