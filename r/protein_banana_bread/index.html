Protein Banana Bread

Description

A moist, sliceable banana-chocolate-chip loaf made with Greek yoghurt, protein powder, and oat flour. Doubles as breakfast. Makes a 10-slice loaf.

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 10
  • Cuisine: dessert
  • Primary Protein: dairy
  • Calories: 190 per serving
  • Protein: 10g
  • Carbs: 28g
  • Fat: 5g

Ingredients

  • 360 g banana (Very ripe — the riper the sweeter. About 3 medium bananas.)
  • 150 g greek yoghurt plain (Plain; keeps it moist and adds protein.)
  • 2 count egg whole
  • 60 g whey protein vanilla (Vanilla. Casein/blend bakes softer than isolate.)
  • 150 g oat flour
  • 60 ml maple syrup
  • 1.5 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp cinnamon ground
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 60 g chocolate chips
  • 0.5 tsp salt

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Preheat the oven to 175°C (350°F) and line a loaf tin with baking paper.
  2. Step 2: Mash the bananas, then whisk in the Greek yoghurt, eggs, maple syrup, and vanilla.
  3. Step 3: Stir in the protein powder, oat flour, baking powder, cinnamon, and salt until just combined, then fold in most of the chocolate chips.
  4. Step 4: Pour into the tin, top with the remaining chips, and bake 40-50 minutes until a skewer comes out clean.
  5. Step 5: Cool in the tin, then slice into 10.
