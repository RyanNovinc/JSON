Teriyaki Chicken Rice Bowl

Description

A fast one-pan teriyaki chicken bowl over jasmine rice with steamed broccoli, built for bulking — 200g chicken per portion over a honey-glaze rice base. Makes 4 meal-prep portions.

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 4
  • Cuisine: asian
  • Primary Protein: chicken
  • Calories: 890 per serving
  • Protein: 56g
  • Carbs: 108g
  • Fat: 18g

Ingredients

  • 800 g chicken breast (Cut into bite-size pieces. 200g per serving.)
  • 370 g jasmine rice (2 cups uncooked.)
  • 28 g olive oil (For browning the chicken.)
  • 120 ml soy sauce (1/2 cup.)
  • 126 g honey
  • 45 ml rice vinegar
  • 3 tsp ginger fresh (Grated.)
  • 4 cloves garlic clove (Minced.)
  • 12 g cornstarch
  • 30 ml water
  • 5 ml sesame oil
  • 500 g broccoli (Florets.)
  • 4 count spring onion (Sliced, to finish.)
  • 18 g sesame seeds (To finish.)

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Rinse the jasmine rice until the water runs clear, then cook per packet and keep warm.
  2. Step 2: Whisk the soy, honey, rice vinegar, ginger, garlic, cornstarch, water, and sesame oil into a smooth glaze with no lumps.
  3. Step 3: Steam the broccoli in a lidded pan with a splash of water until crisp-tender, then drain and set aside.
  4. Step 4: Wipe the pan dry, heat the oil over medium-high, and brown the chicken in a single layer until golden and cooked through, turning once.
  5. Step 5: Pour the glaze over the chicken and simmer, stirring, until glossy and clinging.
  6. Step 6: Divide rice across 4 containers, top with chicken and broccoli, and finish with spring onions and sesame seeds.
