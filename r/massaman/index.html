Slow-Cooked Massaman Beef Curry

Description

Slow-cooked beef and potato in fragrant Thai coconut curry.

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 8
  • Cuisine: thai
  • Primary Protein: beef
  • Calories: 890 per serving
  • Protein: 52g
  • Carbs: 42g
  • Fat: 58g

Ingredients

  • 1600 g beef chuck (Cut into 4cm cubes. Ask the butcher for chuck steak or gravy beef.)
  • 200 g massaman curry paste (Two 114g cans of Maesri, or one jar of Valcom or Ayam.)
  • 800 ml coconut cream (Two 400ml cans of Ayam or Trang.)
  • 500 ml beef stock liquid (Or 500ml water + 1 stock cube.)
  • 200 g brown onion (Approximately 1 large onion, sliced.)
  • 4 cloves garlic clove (Minced.)
  • 1 tsp ginger fresh (Grated.)
  • 800 g potato waxy (Peeled and cut into 3cm chunks. Do not substitute with starchy baking potatoes — they fall apart.)
  • 45 ml fish sauce (Approximately 3 tbsp. Squid brand.)
  • 30 g sugar brown palm substitute (Approximately 2 tbsp.)
  • 30 g tamarind paste (Approximately 2 tbsp.)
  • 30 g olive oil (For browning.)
  • 1 count cinnamon stick
  • 2 count star anise
  • 3 count bay leaves dried

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Heat 15ml of the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Brown the beef in 2-3 batches, about 2 minutes per side to get a hard sear — don't crowd the pan. Transfer to the slow cooker as you go. Browning is optional but adds real depth of flavour.
  2. Step 2: In the same skillet (don't wipe out), drop the heat to medium. Add the sliced onion and cook 3 minutes until starting to soften. Add the garlic, ginger, and massaman curry paste. Cook 2 minutes, stirring constantly, until the paste is fragrant and the oil starts to split out — this "blooms" the paste and is the most important flavour step.
  3. Step 3: Pour in 1 cup of the coconut cream and stir to combine with the paste, scraping all the brown bits off the bottom of the pan. Bring to a simmer for 1 minute, then transfer everything to the slow cooker.
  4. Step 4: Add the remaining coconut cream, beef stock, potatoes, fish sauce, brown sugar, tamarind paste, cinnamon stick, star anise, and bay leaves to the slow cooker. Stir to combine.
  5. Step 5: Cook on LOW for 8 hours or HIGH for 5 hours, until the beef is fork-tender and falls apart with light pressure.
  6. Step 6: Optional finishing reduction: if the sauce looks too thin (slow cookers vary), transfer to a saucepan and simmer uncovered for 10 minutes to reduce. Or mash 2-3 potato chunks gently into the sauce to thicken naturally.
  7. Step 7: Remove the cinnamon stick, star anise, and bay leaves before serving.
