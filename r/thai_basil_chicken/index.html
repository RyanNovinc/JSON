Thai Basil Chicken

Description

Chicken mince stir-fried with garlic, chilli, and holy basil in a salty-sweet sauce, over jasmine rice. Fast, punchy, high-protein.

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 4
  • Cuisine: thai
  • Primary Protein: chicken
  • Calories: 736 per serving
  • Protein: 44g
  • Carbs: 84g
  • Fat: 25g

Ingredients

  • 800 g chicken mince (200g/serve.)
  • 360 g jasmine rice (90g dry/serve.)
  • 40 g thai basil fresh (Added at the end.)
  • 6 cloves garlic clove
  • 20 g red chilli fresh (To taste.)
  • 60 ml oyster sauce
  • 40 ml soy sauce
  • 20 ml fish sauce
  • 12 g sugar white
  • 28 g olive oil
  • 200 g green beans (Optional veg.)

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Cook the jasmine rice.
  2. Step 2: Mix the sauce: oyster sauce, soy, fish sauce, sugar.
  3. Step 3: Stir-fry garlic and chilli, then the chicken mince over high heat until browned.
  4. Step 4: Add the sauce, toss, then fold through the basil off the heat. Serve over rice.
