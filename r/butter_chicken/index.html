Butter Chicken with Basmati Rice

Description

Spiced tomato-cream curry with tender marinated chicken thigh.

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 1
  • Cuisine: indian
  • Primary Protein: chicken
  • Calories: 490 per serving
  • Protein: 48g
  • Carbs: 14g
  • Fat: 28g

Ingredients

  • 200 g chicken thigh skinless
  • 60 g greek yoghurt plain (for marinade)
  • 2 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp ground coriander
  • 0.5 tsp ground turmeric
  • 1 tsp kashmiri chilli powder
  • 2 cloves garlic clove
  • 1 tsp ginger fresh
  • 15 g ghee
  • 75 g brown onion
  • 200 ml tomato passata
  • 60 ml thickened cream
  • 0.5 tsp salt
  • 180 g basmati rice cooked
  • 1 tbsp coriander leaves fresh (for garnish)

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Cut chicken thigh into 3 cm cubes. Combine with yoghurt, half the garam masala, half the cumin, and half the turmeric. Marinate for 20 minutes.
  2. Step 2: Heat ghee in a large frying pan over medium-high heat. Add the marinated chicken and sear for 4-5 minutes until golden on all sides. Remove and set aside.
  3. Step 3: In the same pan, add the diced onion and cook for 3 minutes until softened. Add garlic and ginger and cook for another 30 seconds.
  4. Step 4: Add the remaining garam masala, cumin, coriander, turmeric, and Kashmiri chilli powder. Stir for 30 seconds until fragrant.
  5. Step 5: Pour in the tomato passata and add salt. Bring to a simmer and cook for 5 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  6. Step 6: Return the chicken to the pan and stir through the thickened cream. Simmer for 5 more minutes until the chicken is cooked through and the sauce has thickened.
  7. Step 7: Serve over cooked basmati rice, garnished with fresh coriander leaves.
