Beef Ragù with Gnocchi

Description

Cheap tough beef seared and slow-braised 2-3 hrs in a rich tomato-soffritto sauce until it shreds, tossed through pillowy gnocchi and finished with parmesan. The library's only slow-braise and its best meal-prepper.

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 4
  • Cuisine: italian
  • Primary Protein: beef
  • Calories: 856 per serving
  • Protein: 53g
  • Carbs: 88g
  • Fat: 31g

Ingredients

  • 800 g beef chuck (Whole chuck/blade. ~200g/serve. Braising renders fat you can skim.)
  • 800 g gnocchi (200g/serve; cook fresh to packet.)
  • 700 g crushed tomatoes canned
  • 50 g tomato paste (Browned for depth.)
  • 150 g brown onion
  • 4 cloves garlic clove
  • 120 g carrot (Soffritto; optional.)
  • 100 g celery (Soffritto; optional.)
  • 10 g beef stock cube (Made up to ~250ml braising liquid.)
  • 125 ml red wine cooking (Optional; deglaze. Keep accessible — not required.)
  • 18 g olive oil
  • 40 g parmesan grated (To finish.)
  • 1 tsp oregano dried
  • 1 tsp thyme dried
  • 2 count bay leaves dried
  • 1.5 tsp salt
  • 0.5 tsp black pepper ground

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Pat the beef dry, season, and sear hard on all sides until deeply browned; remove.
  2. Step 2: Soften the soffritto, then brown the tomato paste.
  3. Step 3: Deglaze (optional wine), add tomatoes, stock, herbs; return the beef.
  4. Step 4: Cover and braise low until the beef shreds — oven 160°C ~2.5 hrs (or stovetop low / slow-cooker 8 hrs low).
  5. Step 5: Shred the beef, return to the sauce, skim fat if you like, simmer 15-20 min to thicken.
  6. Step 6: Cook the gnocchi to packet and toss through with a splash of pasta water; finish with parmesan and basil.
