Protein Ice Cream

Description

Thick, creamy high-protein ice cream blended from milk, protein powder, and a little instant pudding mix, then frozen — no ice cream machine needed.

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 1
  • Cuisine: dessert
  • Primary Protein: dairy
  • Calories: 320 per serving
  • Protein: 40g
  • Carbs: 32g
  • Fat: 4g

Ingredients

  • 360 ml full cream milk (Lite/skim for leaner macros, or high-protein milk for more protein.)
  • 35 g whey protein vanilla (About 1 to 1.5 scoops. Vanilla or chocolate.)
  • 12 g instant pudding mix (1 tablespoon — the anti-icy thickener.)
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Blend the milk, protein powder, pudding mix, and vanilla until completely smooth.
  2. Step 2: Pour into a shallow freezer-safe container and freeze for at least 4-6 hours until firm.
  3. Step 3: Let it sit at room temperature for 5-10 minutes to soften, then scoop and serve.
