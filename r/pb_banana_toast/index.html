PB Banana Toast

Description

Toasted sourdough with peanut butter and a whey-spiked mashed-banana spread, finished with honey and chia.

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 1
  • Cuisine: breakfast
  • Primary Protein: dairy
  • Calories: 840 per serving
  • Protein: 45g
  • Carbs: 113g
  • Fat: 26g

Ingredients

  • 120 g sourdough crusty (2-3 slices.)
  • 40 g peanut butter natural (Approximately 2.5 tbsp.)
  • 120 g banana (1 medium.)
  • 25 g whey protein vanilla (Mashed into the banana so it spreads smooth.)
  • 14 g honey (Approximately 1/2 tbsp.)
  • 8 g chia seeds

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Toast the sourdough.
  2. Step 2: Mash the banana in a bowl with a fork, sprinkle in the whey, and mash again until smooth and combined.
  3. Step 3: Spread the peanut butter over the toast, then spread the banana-whey mash on top.
  4. Step 4: Drizzle with honey and sprinkle over the chia seeds.
