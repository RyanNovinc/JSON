Dried Fruit

Description

A small handful of dried fruit — concentrated, fast carbs in a tiny portable package. Great quick energy around training.

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 1
  • Cuisine: snack
  • Primary Protein: plant
  • Calories: 130 per serving
  • Protein: 1g
  • Carbs: 31g

Ingredients

  • 40 g dried fruit (Raisins, apricots, dates, cranberries. Calorie-dense — a small handful is a serving.)

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Grab a small handful of dried fruit and eat — quick, concentrated carbs.
