Honey Soy Salmon Noodle Bowl

Description

Salmon in a glossy honey-soy-garlic-ginger glaze over noodles with roasted broccoli. A sweet-savoury Asian counterpart to the Mediterranean sheet-pan salmon — completely different profile. Makes 4.

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 4
  • Cuisine: asian
  • Primary Protein: fish
  • Calories: 820 per serving
  • Protein: 53g
  • Carbs: 75g
  • Fat: 33g

Ingredients

  • 800 g salmon fillet (Skin off, cubed or whole. 200g per serving.)
  • 280 g noodles (70g dry per serve; egg/soba/udon/rice — any noodle works.)
  • 80 g honey (Glaze; main carb driver.)
  • 60 ml soy sauce (Glaze.)
  • 3 cloves garlic clove (Glaze, minced.)
  • 3 tsp ginger fresh (Glaze, grated. ~1 tbsp.)
  • 10 ml sesame oil (Glaze + noodles. ~2 tsp.)
  • 14 g olive oil (For roasting/searing. ~1 tbsp.)
  • 400 g broccoli (Roasted alongside; snap peas/brussels swap in.)
  • 30 ml rice vinegar (Splash in the glaze. ~2 tbsp.)
  • 6 g sesame seeds (Garnish.)
  • 2 count spring onion (Garnish, sliced.)

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Make the glaze: honey, soy, garlic, ginger, sesame oil, and a splash of rice vinegar.
  2. Step 2: Toss the salmon in some glaze and roast on a sheet pan about 12-14 minutes at 200°C (or pan-sear), basting with more glaze partway. Roast the broccoli alongside.
  3. Step 3: Cook the noodles, then toss with a little sesame oil and the remaining glaze.
  4. Step 4: Build the bowl: noodles, salmon, broccoli; garnish with sesame seeds and spring onion.
