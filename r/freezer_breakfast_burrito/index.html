Freezer Breakfast Burrito

Description

A batch of 5 hearty sausage, egg, cheese, and potato burritos to wrap and freeze. Macros are per burrito.

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 5
  • Cuisine: breakfast
  • Primary Protein: pork
  • Calories: 680 per serving
  • Protein: 37g
  • Carbs: 48g
  • Fat: 37g

Ingredients

  • 350 g breakfast sausage (Browned and crumbled.)
  • 10 count egg whole
  • 5 count tortilla large
  • 150 g cheese tasty grated
  • 250 g potato waxy (Diced small for hash.)
  • 120 g brown onion (Diced.)
  • 120 g capsicum red (Diced.)
  • 11 g olive oil
  • 0.5 tsp salt
  • 0.5 tsp black pepper ground

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Dice and fry the potato in the olive oil over medium-high heat for 10-12 minutes until tender, then add the onion and capsicum and cook until soft. Set aside.
  2. Step 2: Brown the breakfast sausage in the pan, breaking it up, until cooked through.
  3. Step 3: Whisk the eggs with salt and pepper and scramble softly.
  4. Step 4: Lay out the tortillas, divide the sausage, eggs, potato-veg mix, and cheese between them, and roll up tightly.
  5. Step 5: Eat one now; wrap the rest individually in foil and freeze. Reheat from frozen in the microwave, then crisp in a pan.
