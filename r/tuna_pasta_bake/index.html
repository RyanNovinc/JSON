Tuna Pasta Bake

Description

Pasta and canned tuna in a creamy high-protein cheese sauce (blended cottage cheese + milk + cheddar) with peas and sweetcorn, baked golden under a cheese top. The cheapest, most pantry-friendly meal on the list — no fresh shopping needed. Makes 4.

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 4
  • Cuisine: australian
  • Primary Protein: fish
  • Calories: 795 per serving
  • Protein: 57g
  • Carbs: 91g
  • Fat: 22g

Ingredients

  • 370 g tuna (About 2 large cans, drained. ~90g drained per serving.)
  • 320 g macaroni (80g dry per serve; penne/spiral/macaroni — any short shape.)
  • 300 g cottage cheese (Blended into the sauce — the protein-rich base.)
  • 250 ml full cream milk
  • 130 g cheese tasty grated (Grated cheddar/tasty; sauce + top.)
  • 2 cloves garlic clove (Optional if using fresh.)
  • 150 g brown onion (Optional; soften first. ~1 medium onion.)
  • 150 g peas (Frozen; broccoli swaps in.)
  • 150 g sweetcorn
  • 14 g olive oil (1 tbsp for softening aromatics.)
  • 40 g breadcrumbs (Optional crunchy top.)
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 0.5 tsp black pepper ground

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Cook the pasta to just under al dente, then drain.
  2. Step 2: Make the sauce: blend the cottage cheese with the milk, warm through, then melt in most of the grated cheese with the herbs, salt, and pepper (soften the garlic and onion in oil first if using fresh).
  3. Step 3: Fold through the drained tuna, peas, sweetcorn, and pasta.
  4. Step 4: Tip into a baking dish and top with the remaining cheese (and breadcrumbs if using).
  5. Step 5: Bake about 20 minutes at 200°C until golden and bubbling.
