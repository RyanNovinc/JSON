Beef Bulgogi Bowl

Description

Korean BBQ beef — thinly sliced beef in a sweet-savoury soy-sesame-garlic marinade, seared hot until caramelised, over jasmine rice with spring onion and sesame. A sweeter, nuttier counterpart to the beef & broccoli stir-fry.

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 4
  • Cuisine: asian
  • Primary Protein: beef
  • Calories: 820 per serving
  • Protein: 50g
  • Carbs: 96g
  • Fat: 22g

Ingredients

  • 800 g sirloin steak (Sliced thin against the grain; freeze ~1hr first for easier slicing. 200g per serving.)
  • 370 g jasmine rice (2 cups uncooked.)
  • 60 ml soy sauce (Marinade.)
  • 24 g brown sugar (Marinade; honey works too.)
  • 15 ml sesame oil (Marinade.)
  • 4 cloves garlic clove (Minced, into marinade.)
  • 75 g brown onion (Grated into the marinade.)
  • 0.5 tsp black pepper ground (Marinade.)
  • 9 g sesame seeds (Half in marinade, half to garnish.)
  • 0.5 count pear (Optional tenderiser, grated into marinade; or apple, or omit.)
  • 1 tsp ginger fresh (Optional, grated into marinade.)
  • 14 g olive oil (For searing.)
  • 2 count spring onion (Sliced, to garnish.)

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Slice the beef thinly against the grain (freezing it about 1 hour first makes this much easier).
  2. Step 2: Mix the marinade: soy sauce, brown sugar, sesame oil, minced garlic, grated onion, black pepper, and half the sesame seeds (plus grated pear and ginger if using).
  3. Step 3: Toss the beef in the marinade and rest 30 minutes to overnight in the fridge.
  4. Step 4: Rinse the jasmine rice until the water runs clear, then cook per packet and keep warm.
  5. Step 5: Sear the beef in a little olive oil in a hot pan, in batches so it caramelises rather than stews, for 2-3 minutes.
  6. Step 6: Serve the beef over jasmine rice, garnished with spring onion and the remaining sesame seeds.
