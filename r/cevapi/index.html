Ćevapi

Description

Grilled garlicky-paprika minced-meat sausages in warm flatbread with raw onion and ajvar (red pepper relish) — the traditional Balkan serve.

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 4
  • Cuisine: mediterranean
  • Primary Protein: beef
  • Calories: 806 per serving
  • Protein: 51g
  • Carbs: 71g
  • Fat: 35g

Ingredients

  • 500 g beef mince regular (Lean beef preferred; part of the blend. ~200g total mince/serve.)
  • 300 g lamb mince (The authentic blend is beef + lamb (+ pork). All-beef or beef-pork works everywhere; lamb/pork raise fat.)
  • 150 g brown onion (Grated into the mix — key for moisture (raw red onion to serve).)
  • 6 cloves garlic clove (Heavy hand — the signature.)
  • 4 tsp paprika sweet (Sweet or smoked — the heart of the flavour.)
  • 0.5 tsp bicarb soda (Springy-tender texture — do not skip.)
  • 160 g ajvar (To serve.)
  • 150 g cherry tomatoes (Salad (rice plate).)
  • 150 g cucumber (Salad (rice plate).)
  • 1.5 tsp salt
  • 0.5 tsp black pepper ground

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Mix the mince with grated onion, garlic, paprika, salt, pepper, bicarb (and a splash of sparkling water), then rest.
  2. Step 2: With damp hands, roll into small fingers ~8 cm long.
  3. Step 3: Grill or pan-sear over medium-high ~3-4 min per side, turning once, until browned with a crust but juicy. Do not press.
  4. Step 4: Serve per plate (flatbread or rice plate) with onion and ajvar.
