Slow-Simmered Bolognese

Description

Slow-simmered beef and tomato ragù with red wine and pancetta.

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 8
  • Cuisine: italian
  • Primary Protein: beef
  • Calories: 510 per serving
  • Protein: 32g
  • Carbs: 18g
  • Fat: 32g

Ingredients

  • 30 g olive oil (Split: 15g for sautéing the soffritto, 15g for browning the mince.)
  • 1000 g beef mince regular (Don't use lean — fat is doing real work here.)
  • 100 g pancetta diced
  • 300 g brown onion (Approximately 2 medium onions, finely diced.)
  • 4 cloves garlic clove (Minced.)
  • 120 g carrot (Approximately 1 medium, finely diced.)
  • 100 g celery (Approximately 2 stalks, finely diced.)
  • 250 ml red wine cooking (Cabernet sauvignon or merlot. Any cleanskin works.)
  • 1600 g crushed tomatoes canned (Two 800g tins.)
  • 60 g tomato paste (Approximately 4 tbsp.)
  • 30 g beef stock cube (Three cubes.)
  • 20 ml worcestershire sauce (Approximately 1 tbsp.)
  • 8 g sugar white (Approximately 2 tsp. Balances tomato acidity.)
  • 2 tsp oregano dried
  • 2 tsp thyme dried
  • 3 count bay leaves dried
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 0.5 tsp black pepper ground

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Heat 15g of the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the diced onion, carrot, and celery. Cook for 7 minutes until soft and the onion is translucent. Add the garlic and pancetta; cook 2 more minutes. Transfer everything to the slow cooker.
  2. Step 2: Add the remaining 15g oil to the same skillet over high heat. Add the beef mince and brown in two batches if needed — don't overcrowd the pan. Break up the mince with a wooden spoon as it cooks. Transfer to the slow cooker.
  3. Step 3: Return the skillet to medium heat. Pour in the red wine and scrape all the browned bits from the bottom of the pan — this fond is where the flavour lives. Simmer for 2 minutes, then pour into the slow cooker.
  4. Step 4: Add the crushed tomatoes, tomato paste, stock cubes, Worcestershire sauce, sugar, oregano, thyme, bay leaves, salt, and pepper to the slow cooker. Stir well.
  5. Step 5: Cook on LOW for 6 hours (or HIGH for 4 hours).
  6. Step 6: After cooking, taste and adjust salt. Remove the bay leaves. If the sauce looks too liquid (common in slow cookers — they don't reduce much), prop the lid open with a wooden spoon and cook on HIGH for 30 more minutes.
