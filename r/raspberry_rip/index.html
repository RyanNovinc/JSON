Raspberry Rip

Description

Raspberry, chocolate, and coconut — tastes like a Cherry Ripe bar in liquid form

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 1
  • Cuisine: smoothie
  • Primary Protein: dairy
  • Calories: 1070 per serving
  • Protein: 54g
  • Carbs: 103g
  • Fat: 52g

Ingredients

  • 300 ml full cream milk
  • 100 ml coconut cream
  • 125 g raspberries frozen (Approximately 1 cup.)
  • 120 g banana (Frozen, peeled.)
  • 200 g greek yoghurt vanilla full fat
  • 30 g whey protein chocolate (1 scoop.)
  • 10 g cocoa powder (Approximately 2 tbsp.)
  • 6 g desiccated coconut (Approximately 1 tbsp.)
  • 21 g honey (Approximately 1 tbsp.)

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Add all ingredients to a high-powered blender.
  2. Step 2: Blend on high for 45-60 seconds until smooth. The raspberry seeds will leave a subtle texture — that's fine.
  3. Step 3: Pour into a tall glass. The flavour is essentially a Cherry Ripe in liquid form — chocolate, coconut, raspberry.
