Slow-Cooked BBQ Pulled Pork

Description

Slow-cooked pork shoulder shredded and tossed in tangy BBQ sauce.

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 8
  • Cuisine: australian
  • Primary Protein: pork
  • Calories: 610 per serving
  • Protein: 48g
  • Carbs: 28g
  • Fat: 32g

Ingredients

  • 2200 g pork shoulder boneless
  • 180 ml apple juice cloudy (Pour around the pork in the slow cooker, not over the rub.)
  • 24 g brown sugar (For the rub. Approximately 2 tbsp.)
  • 2 tsp paprika sweet (For the rub.)
  • 1 tsp onion powder (For the rub.)
  • 1 tsp garlic powder (For the rub.)
  • 0.5 tsp ground cumin (For the rub.)
  • 0.75 tsp mustard powder (For the rub.)
  • 1 tsp salt (For the rub. Approximately 6 g.)
  • 0.5 tsp black pepper ground (For the rub.)
  • 625 g ketchup tomato (For the BBQ sauce. Approximately 2½ cups.)
  • 24 g brown sugar (For the BBQ sauce. Approximately 2 tbsp.)
  • 17 ml worcestershire sauce (For the BBQ sauce. Approximately 1 tbsp.)
  • 120 ml apple cider vinegar (For the BBQ sauce. Approximately ½ cup.)
  • 15 ml lemon juice (For the BBQ sauce. Approximately 1 tbsp.)
  • 1.5 tsp black pepper ground (For the BBQ sauce.)
  • 1.5 tsp onion powder (For the BBQ sauce.)
  • 1.5 tsp mustard powder (For the BBQ sauce.)

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Mix the rub ingredients (24g brown sugar, paprika, onion powder, garlic powder, cumin, mustard powder, salt, black pepper) in a small bowl. Rub generously all over the pork shoulder, including the fat cap. If time permits, marinate in the fridge for an hour; otherwise straight to step 2.
  2. Step 2: Place pork in the slow cooker, fat cap facing up. Pour the apple juice around the pork — not over the rub.
  3. Step 3: Cook on low for 8 hours, or high for 4-5 hours. Pork is done when it shreds easily under light fork pressure.
  4. Step 4: Lift the pork carefully into a roasting pan or large bowl. Pour the cooking liquid into a measuring jug. Skim and reserve 1 cup (240ml) of the juices for the BBQ sauce. The rest can be discarded or frozen for stock.
  5. Step 5: (Optional crisp step.) Preheat oven to 180°C. Roast the lifted pork for 20 minutes for surface caramelisation. Worth it for texture; skip if rushed.
  6. Step 6: Make the BBQ sauce (can be done during the slow cook): combine ketchup, the second 24g brown sugar, Worcestershire, apple cider vinegar, reserved pork juices (240ml), lemon juice, and the sauce spices (1.5 tsp each of black pepper, onion powder, mustard powder) in a saucepan. Bring to a simmer over medium heat. Simmer gently for 1 hour, stirring occasionally, until thickened to coating consistency.
  7. Step 7: Shred the pork with two forks, discarding any large gristle pieces. If you're planning the Pulled Pork Tacos plate this week, reserve approximately 151g of plain shredded pork per planned tacos serve in a separate container before continuing.
  8. Step 8: Pour BBQ sauce over the remaining shredded pork and toss to coat generously. You'll have extra sauce — bottle and use it for serving.
