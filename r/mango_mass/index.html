Mango Mass

Description

Tropical mango and nectar with macadamia butter — like drinking a liquid mango smoothie bowl

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 1
  • Cuisine: smoothie
  • Primary Protein: dairy
  • Calories: 1147 per serving
  • Protein: 52g
  • Carbs: 126g
  • Fat: 50g

Ingredients

  • 300 ml full cream milk
  • 165 g mango frozen (Approximately 1 cup.)
  • 120 g banana (Frozen, peeled. Roughly 1 medium banana.)
  • 150 ml mango nectar
  • 200 g greek yoghurt vanilla full fat
  • 30 g whey protein vanilla (1 scoop.)
  • 32 g macadamia butter (Approximately 2 tbsp.)
  • 21 g honey (Approximately 1 tbsp.)

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Add all ingredients to a high-powered blender.
  2. Step 2: Start at low speed for 5 seconds to break up the frozen mango and banana, then increase to high speed.
  3. Step 3: Blend on high for 45-60 seconds until completely smooth.
  4. Step 4: Pour into a large glass. If too thick, add a splash of extra milk and pulse to combine.
