Steak & Eggs

Description

Garlic-butter seared sirloin with fried eggs and crispy pan potatoes — the highest-protein plate in the set.

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 1
  • Cuisine: breakfast
  • Primary Protein: beef
  • Calories: 820 per serving
  • Protein: 65g
  • Carbs: 37g
  • Fat: 44g

Ingredients

  • 200 g sirloin steak
  • 3 count egg whole
  • 200 g potato waxy (Diced small.)
  • 12 g butter salted (For searing the steak.)
  • 7 g olive oil (For the potatoes.)
  • 2 cloves garlic clove (Crushed, for the steak butter.)
  • 0.5 tsp salt
  • 0.5 tsp black pepper ground

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Pat the steak dry, season both sides with salt and pepper, and leave it to come up to room temperature.
  2. Step 2: Dice the potatoes and pan-fry in the olive oil over medium-high heat for 12-15 minutes until golden and tender, then season and set aside.
  3. Step 3: Sear the steak in the butter with the crushed garlic over high heat, 2-3 minutes per side for medium, then rest it for 5 minutes.
  4. Step 4: Fry the eggs in the same pan, slice the rested steak, and plate everything together.
