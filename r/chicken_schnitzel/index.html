Chicken Schnitzel

Description

Plain crumbed chicken schnitzel with salad or potatoes, hot or cold, lemon to serve. The lean, simple option.

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 4
  • Cuisine: australian
  • Primary Protein: chicken
  • Calories: 621 per serving
  • Protein: 54g
  • Carbs: 53g
  • Fat: 20g

Ingredients

  • 800 g chicken breast (Sliced thin/pounded to ~1/2cm. 200g/serve.)
  • 40 g flour plain (Seasoned, for dredging.)
  • 2 count egg whole (Beaten.)
  • 100 g breadcrumbs (Panko = crispier.)
  • 55 g olive oil (Shallow-fry; ~12g absorbed/serve (most drains). Oven-bake w/ spray for ~10g less fat/serve.)
  • 1 tsp garlic powder (In the crumb/flour.)
  • 1 tsp paprika sweet (In the crumb/flour.)
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 0.5 tsp black pepper ground

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Slice the chicken breast thin, or pound to ~1/2 cm.
  2. Step 2: Crumb: seasoned flour -> beaten egg -> breadcrumbs, pressing on, then rest 15-20 min so the coating sets.
  3. Step 3: Shallow-fry in oil over medium-high, 3-4 min per side, until golden and 73C/165F inside; drain on a rack.
  4. Step 4: Assemble per plate: plate, roll, or parma.
