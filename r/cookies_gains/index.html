Cookies & Gains

Description

Oreo cookies blended with vanilla and chocolate — like drinking a cookies and cream milkshake

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 1
  • Cuisine: smoothie
  • Primary Protein: dairy
  • Calories: 1148 per serving
  • Protein: 56g
  • Carbs: 128g
  • Fat: 47g

Ingredients

  • 300 ml full cream milk
  • 120 g banana (Frozen, peeled. Roughly 1 medium banana.)
  • 200 g greek yoghurt vanilla full fat
  • 30 g whey protein chocolate (1 scoop.)
  • 44 g oreo cookies (Approximately 4 cookies. Reserve one for crushing over the top.)
  • 16 g peanut butter natural (Approximately 1 tbsp.)
  • 21 g honey (Approximately 1 tbsp.)
  • 50 g ice cream vanilla

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Add the milk and yoghurt to the blender first.
  2. Step 2: Add the banana, whey, 3 of the Oreos, peanut butter, honey, and ice cream.
  3. Step 3: Blend on high for 45-60 seconds until smooth. Some small Oreo flecks are fine — they add texture.
  4. Step 4: Pour into a tall glass. Crush the reserved Oreo and sprinkle over the top.
