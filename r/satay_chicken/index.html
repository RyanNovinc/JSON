Satay Chicken

Description

Velveted chicken seared with charred onion in a creamy peanut-coconut satay sauce, over jasmine rice. Aussie-Chinese-restaurant style with a hint of curry powder (not Thai skewer satay). Rich and calorie-dense from the peanut butter and coconut milk. Makes 4.

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 4
  • Cuisine: asian
  • Primary Protein: chicken
  • Calories: 919 per serving
  • Protein: 50g
  • Carbs: 79g
  • Fat: 45g

Ingredients

  • 800 g chicken thigh (Default cut (juicier). Swap chicken_breast for leaner/lower-fat. 200g per serving. Sliced.)
  • 300 g jasmine rice (About 75g dry per serve.)
  • 80 g peanut butter natural (Smooth natural; base of the sauce. Fat-dense — counts a lot.)
  • 150 ml coconut milk (Full-fat canned; about a third of a 400ml can. Fat-dense.)
  • 45 ml soy sauce (~1 tbsp to velvet + ~2 tbsp in the sauce.)
  • 18 g brown sugar (Balances the sauce.)
  • 3 tsp curry powder (KEY — the Chinese-restaurant satay flavour. Do not omit.)
  • 3 cloves garlic clove (Minced.)
  • 150 g brown onion (Sliced, charred into the sauce.)
  • 14 g olive oil (For searing.)
  • 6 g cornstarch (For velveting the chicken.)
  • 1 count lime (To finish.)

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Rinse the jasmine rice until the water runs clear, then cook per packet and keep warm.
  2. Step 2: Velvet the sliced chicken: toss with about 1 tbsp soy sauce and the cornstarch, then rest while you prep.
  3. Step 3: Mix the satay sauce: peanut butter, coconut milk, the remaining soy, brown sugar, curry powder, and a splash of water until smooth.
  4. Step 4: Sear the chicken in a hot pan or wok until browned, then remove.
  5. Step 5: Char the onion and garlic in the same pan until soft with golden edges.
  6. Step 6: Return the chicken, pour in the sauce, and simmer until glossy and clinging; finish with a squeeze of lime.
  7. Step 7: Serve over the jasmine rice, garnished with crushed peanuts and coriander.
