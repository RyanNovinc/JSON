Frozen Date Snickers Bark

Description

The viral frozen date bark — pressed Medjool dates, a peanut-butter-and-protein layer, chopped peanuts, and a chocolate top. Tastes like a Snickers. Breaks into ~10 pieces.

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 10
  • Cuisine: dessert
  • Primary Protein: dairy
  • Calories: 190 per serving
  • Protein: 6g
  • Carbs: 26g
  • Fat: 9g

Ingredients

  • 250 g medjool dates (Pitted, soft. The chewy caramel-like base.)
  • 80 g peanut butter natural (Warmed to spread.)
  • 20 g whey protein vanilla (Stirred into the peanut butter for the higher-protein version.)
  • 40 g peanuts roasted unsalted (Roughly chopped, for crunch.)
  • 80 g chocolate chips (Melted for the top.)
  • 0.12 tsp salt (Flaky, sprinkled on top.)

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Line a tray with baking paper, then press the pitted dates into one solid overlapping layer.
  2. Step 2: Stir the protein powder into the warmed peanut butter, spread over the dates, and press in the chopped peanuts.
  3. Step 3: Melt the chocolate chips, spread over the top, and sprinkle with flaky salt.
  4. Step 4: Freeze 20-30 minutes until set, then break into about 10 pieces.
