Energy Lift Heavy

Description

Caffeinated pre-workout smoothie with mango and chia — energy and nutrition combined

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 1
  • Cuisine: smoothie
  • Primary Protein: dairy
  • Calories: 961 per serving
  • Protein: 52g
  • Carbs: 108g
  • Fat: 36g

Ingredients

  • 300 ml full cream milk
  • 120 g banana (Frozen, peeled.)
  • 165 g mango frozen (Approximately 1 cup.)
  • 200 g greek yoghurt vanilla full fat
  • 30 g whey protein vanilla (1 scoop.)
  • 30 ml coconut cream (Approximately 2 tbsp.)
  • 21 g honey (Approximately 1 tbsp.)
  • 30 ml espresso shot (1 shot, cooled. Substitute: 1 tsp instant coffee dissolved in 30ml hot water then cooled.)
  • 12 g chia seeds (Approximately 1 tbsp.)

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Brew the espresso first and let it cool while you measure the other ingredients (or use leftover cold espresso).
  2. Step 2: Add all ingredients to a high-powered blender.
  3. Step 3: Blend on high for 45-60 seconds until smooth. The chia seeds will thicken the smoothie if it sits — drink within 5 minutes.
  4. Step 4: Best consumed 30-45 minutes before training.
