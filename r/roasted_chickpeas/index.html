Roasted Chickpeas

Description

Crunchy oven-roasted chickpeas — a high-fibre plant snack with a satisfying crisp. Roast a can, or buy them ready-roasted.

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 3
  • Cuisine: snack
  • Primary Protein: plant
  • Calories: 190 per serving
  • Protein: 10g
  • Carbs: 22g
  • Fat: 7g

Ingredients

  • 400 g chickpeas (One drained can. Or skip cooking and buy a bag of ready-roasted chickpeas.)
  • 15 g olive oil (About 1 tbsp.)
  • 0.5 tsp salt (Plus any spices you like — paprika, cumin.)

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Preheat the oven to 200°C (400°F). Drain, rinse, and thoroughly dry the chickpeas.
  2. Step 2: Toss with the oil and salt, spread on a tray, and roast 25-30 minutes, shaking halfway, until crunchy.
  3. Step 3: Cool (they crisp up further as they cool) and store in an airtight container.
