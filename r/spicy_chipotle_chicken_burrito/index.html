Spicy Chipotle Chicken Burrito

Description

Smoky chipotle-spiced chicken thigh with rice, black beans, cheese, and salsa wrapped in a large tortilla. Default heat from smoked paprika, chilli powder, and cayenne.

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 4
  • Cuisine: mexican
  • Primary Protein: chicken
  • Calories: 933 per serving
  • Protein: 56g
  • Carbs: 101g
  • Fat: 32g

Ingredients

  • 800 g chicken thigh skinless (200g/serve; breast swaps in leaner.)
  • 260 g jasmine rice (65g dry/serve.)
  • 240 g black beans (One drained can.)
  • 80 g cheese tasty grated
  • 150 g cherry tomatoes (Salsa.)
  • 150 g brown onion (Red onion for salsa.)
  • 14 g olive oil
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 tsp paprika smoked (Smoked preferred.)
  • 2 tsp chilli powder
  • 1 tsp cayenne pepper (Default heat.)
  • 1 count lime
  • 12 g coriander fresh
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 0.5 tsp black pepper ground

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Cook the rice; stir through lime and coriander.
  2. Step 2: Spice and cook the chicken (cumin, smoked paprika, chilli, cayenne).
  3. Step 3: Warm the beans and make a quick salsa.
  4. Step 4: Assemble per plate (burrito, extra hot, or bowl).
