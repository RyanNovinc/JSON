Chocolate Protein Mousse

Description

Rich, silky chocolate mousse made by blending cottage cheese completely smooth with cocoa and chocolate protein powder, then chilling. No cooking.

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 1
  • Cuisine: dessert
  • Primary Protein: dairy
  • Calories: 395 per serving
  • Protein: 38g
  • Carbs: 36g
  • Fat: 14g

Ingredients

  • 200 g cottage cheese (Full-fat or 2% blends silkiest. Blend until no curds remain.)
  • 15 g whey protein chocolate (About 1/2 scoop chocolate; boosts protein and thickens.)
  • 12 g cocoa powder (2 tablespoons.)
  • 15 ml maple syrup (Adjust to taste.)
  • 20 ml full cream milk (To loosen if needed.)
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 10 g chocolate chips (Topping.)
  • 0.06 tsp salt

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Blend the cottage cheese, protein powder, cocoa, maple syrup, milk, vanilla, and salt until completely smooth and mousse-like.
  2. Step 2: Spoon into a serving dish and chill for 30 minutes to thicken.
  3. Step 3: Top with chocolate chips and serve.
