King Kong Chocolate

Description

Chocolate, peanut butter, cream — the pinch of salt makes it taste like a thick shake, not a sad gym drink

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 1
  • Cuisine: smoothie
  • Primary Protein: dairy
  • Calories: 1418 per serving
  • Protein: 85g
  • Carbs: 93g
  • Fat: 79g

Ingredients

  • 300 ml full cream milk
  • 100 ml thickened cream
  • 120 g banana (Frozen, peeled. Roughly 1 medium banana.)
  • 200 g greek yoghurt vanilla full fat
  • 60 g whey protein chocolate (2 scoops.)
  • 10 g cocoa powder (Approximately 2 tbsp.)
  • 32 g peanut butter natural (Approximately 2 tbsp.)
  • 21 g honey (Approximately 1 tbsp.)
  • 0.25 tsp salt (Pinch — enhances the chocolate flavour.)

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Add the milk, cream, and yoghurt to the blender first to give the dry ingredients something to blend into.
  2. Step 2: Add the frozen banana, whey, cocoa powder, peanut butter, honey, and salt.
  3. Step 3: Start at low speed for 5 seconds to break up the frozen banana, then increase to high speed.
  4. Step 4: Blend on high for 60 seconds until completely smooth and silky. The thickened cream should be fully incorporated.
  5. Step 5: Pour into a large glass. The salt is essential — it transforms the flavour from "sad gym shake" to "Maccas thick shake."
