Carne Asada Bowl

Description

Citrus-marinated grilled steak, sliced thin against the grain, over cilantro-lime rice with black beans, corn, pico, and cheese. The first whole-cut beef dish — a charred, citrusy contrast to the soy-glaze beef meals. Makes 4.

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 4
  • Cuisine: mexican
  • Primary Protein: beef
  • Calories: 827 per serving
  • Protein: 62g
  • Carbs: 93g
  • Fat: 23g

Ingredients

  • 800 g sirloin steak (Lean sirloin (default/accessible) or flank/skirt (authentic, fattier). 200g per serving.)
  • 300 g jasmine rice (Cilantro-lime style; ~75g dry/serve.)
  • 240 g black beans canned (One drained can.)
  • 200 g sweetcorn
  • 200 g cherry tomatoes (Pico; or any tomato.)
  • 150 g brown onion (Red onion for the pico. ~1 medium onion.)
  • 2 count lime (Marinade + cilantro-lime rice.)
  • 80 ml orange juice (Citrus marinade base.)
  • 3 cloves garlic clove (Marinade.)
  • 80 g cheese tasty grated (Grated topping; optional.)
  • 14 g olive oil (~1 tbsp.)
  • 2 tsp ground cumin (Marinade.)
  • 2 tsp chilli powder (Marinade.)
  • 1 tsp paprika sweet
  • 5 g brown sugar (Optional pinch, helps the char. ~1 tsp.)
  • 15 g coriander fresh (Rice + pico.)
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 0.5 tsp black pepper ground

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Make the marinade: lime and orange juice, garlic, cumin, chilli powder, oil, salt (and an optional pinch of sugar). Marinate the steak 2-4 hours — not more than 8, as the citrus turns it mushy.
  2. Step 2: Cook the rice, then stir through lime juice, chopped coriander, and salt.
  3. Step 3: Warm the beans and make a quick pico (chopped tomato, red onion, coriander, lime).
  4. Step 4: Grill or sear the steak hot until charred, about 4-7 minutes per side to your doneness, then rest 5-10 minutes and slice thin against the grain (the key tenderness step).
  5. Step 5: Build the bowl: rice, sliced steak, beans, corn, pico, and cheese.
