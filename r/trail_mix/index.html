Trail Mix

Description

A scoop of trail mix — nuts, dried fruit, and chocolate. The portable, calorie-dense grab that mixes protein, carbs, and fat in one handful.

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 1
  • Cuisine: snack
  • Primary Protein: plant
  • Calories: 290 per serving
  • Protein: 8g
  • Carbs: 24g
  • Fat: 18g

Ingredients

  • 30 g mixed nuts
  • 20 g dried fruit (Raisins, cranberries, chopped apricot.)
  • 10 g chocolate chips (Dark chocolate chips or chopped dark chocolate.)

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Grab a handful of trail mix, or mix your own from nuts, dried fruit, and chocolate.
