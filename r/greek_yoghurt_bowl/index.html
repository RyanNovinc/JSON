Greek Yoghurt Power Bowl

Description

A thick protein-yoghurt base topped with berries, chia, and granola — no-cook, 5 minutes.

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 1
  • Cuisine: breakfast
  • Primary Protein: dairy
  • Calories: 740 per serving
  • Protein: 47g
  • Carbs: 91g
  • Fat: 22g

Ingredients

  • 280 g greek yoghurt vanilla full fat
  • 30 g whey protein vanilla (1 scoop, stirred into the yoghurt for a thick protein base.)
  • 21 g honey (Approximately 1 tbsp.)

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Spoon the Greek yoghurt into a bowl, add the whey and honey, and stir until smooth and creamy — loosen with a splash of milk if it gets too thick.
