Banana Bulk

Description

Simple banana-date smoothie with cinnamon — the cheapest bulker in the database

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 1
  • Cuisine: smoothie
  • Primary Protein: dairy
  • Calories: 1302 per serving
  • Protein: 64g
  • Carbs: 152g
  • Fat: 51g

Ingredients

  • 350 ml full cream milk
  • 240 g banana (2 frozen bananas, peeled.)
  • 200 g greek yoghurt vanilla full fat
  • 30 g whey protein vanilla (1 scoop.)
  • 48 g peanut butter natural (Approximately 3 tbsp.)
  • 48 g medjool dates (2 dates, pitted.)
  • 21 g honey (Approximately 1 tbsp.)
  • 1 tsp cinnamon ground

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Pit the dates if needed and roughly chop them — this helps them blend.
  2. Step 2: Add the milk and yoghurt to the blender first.
  3. Step 3: Add the frozen bananas, dates, whey, peanut butter, honey, and cinnamon.
  4. Step 4: Blend on high for 60 seconds until completely smooth — the dates need extra time to fully break down.
  5. Step 5: Pour into a tall glass. The cheapest smoothie in the database — total ingredient cost roughly $3 AUD.
