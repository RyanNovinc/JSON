Chicken Fajita Bowl

Description

Seasoned chicken with peppers and onions over jasmine rice, topped with black beans and lime. Perfect for meal prep.

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 4
  • Cuisine: mexican
  • Primary Protein: chicken
  • Calories: 770 per serving
  • Protein: 56g
  • Carbs: 96g
  • Fat: 14g

Ingredients

  • 800 g chicken breast (Cut into strips. 200g per serving.)
  • 320 g jasmine rice (Uncooked, about 1.5 cups.)
  • 300 g capsicum red (Sliced into strips.)
  • 300 g brown onion (Sliced.)
  • 400 g black beans (1 can, drained and rinsed.)
  • 42 g olive oil (For cooking.)
  • 4 cloves garlic clove (Minced.)
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 tsp chilli powder
  • 1 tsp paprika sweet
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 0.5 tsp black pepper ground
  • 2 count lime (Juice and wedges.)
  • 480 ml water (For cooking rice.)

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Cook the jasmine rice according to package directions (about 18 minutes).
  2. Step 2: Mix the fajita seasoning: combine cumin, chilli powder, paprika, garlic powder, salt, and pepper in a small bowl.
  3. Step 3: Season the chicken strips with half the fajita spice mix.
  4. Step 4: Heat 2 tablespoons olive oil in a large pan over medium-high heat. Cook chicken strips 6-8 minutes until golden and cooked through. Remove and set aside.
  5. Step 5: In the same pan, add remaining oil. Sauté onions and peppers with remaining spice mix for 5-6 minutes until softened.
  6. Step 6: Add garlic and cook 1 minute until fragrant.
  7. Step 7: Return chicken to pan, add black beans, and warm through for 2-3 minutes.
  8. Step 8: Serve over rice with fresh lime juice and lime wedges.
