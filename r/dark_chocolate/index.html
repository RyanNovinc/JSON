Dark Chocolate

Description

A couple of squares of dark chocolate — the calorie-dense treat snack for when you want something sweet without derailing the day.

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 1
  • Cuisine: snack
  • Primary Protein: plant
  • Calories: 170 per serving
  • Protein: 2g
  • Carbs: 13g
  • Fat: 12g

Ingredients

  • 30 g dark chocolate (About 2-3 squares of a 70%+ bar.)

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Break off a couple of squares of dark chocolate and enjoy — 70%+ for less sugar.
