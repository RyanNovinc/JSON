Mixed Nuts

Description

A handful of mixed nuts — one of the most calorie-dense, easy-surplus snacks for a bulk. Almonds, cashews, walnuts and more.

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 1
  • Cuisine: snack
  • Primary Protein: plant
  • Calories: 250 per serving
  • Protein: 9g
  • Carbs: 9g
  • Fat: 22g

Ingredients

  • 40 g mixed nuts (About a small handful. Calorie-dense — easy to overdo, easy to use for surplus.)

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Grab a handful of mixed nuts and eat — unsalted or lightly salted.
