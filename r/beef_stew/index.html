Red Wine Beef Stew

Description

Fork-tender beef chuck with carrots, mushrooms and baby potatoes.

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 6
  • Cuisine: australian
  • Primary Protein: beef
  • Calories: 700 per serving
  • Protein: 46g
  • Carbs: 32g
  • Fat: 38g

Ingredients

  • 1200 g beef chuck (Cut into 3.5cm cubes. Ask the butcher for it pre-cubed if you're rushed.)
  • 50 g flour plain (For dusting the beef. Approximately ⅓ cup.)
  • 45 g olive oil (Split: 30g for browning, 15g for sautéing the vegetables.)
  • 250 g brown onion (Approximately 1 large, halved and sliced 1cm thick.)
  • 4 cloves garlic clove (Minced.)
  • 300 g carrot (Approximately 3 medium, cut into 2.5cm chunks on the diagonal.)
  • 150 g celery (Approximately 2 stalks, cut into 2.5cm chunks.)
  • 250 g mushroom brown (Halved. Optional but recommended for depth of flavour.)
  • 400 g potato baby chat (Halved, skins on.)
  • 500 ml red wine cooking (Full-bodied — cabernet sauvignon, merlot, or shiraz. Discount bottles are fine.)
  • 750 ml beef stock liquid (Salt-reduced preferred. Campbell's Real Stock or Massel.)
  • 30 g tomato paste (Approximately 2 tbsp.)
  • 10 ml worcestershire sauce (Approximately 2 tsp.)
  • 4 count thyme fresh (4 sprigs, tied together for easy removal. Substitute 1 tsp dried thyme if unavailable.)
  • 2 count bay leaves dried
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 0.5 tsp black pepper ground

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Pat the beef cubes dry with paper towel. Season with the salt and pepper. Place the beef and flour in a large zip-top bag or bowl and toss to coat evenly. Shake off the excess.
  2. Step 2: Heat 30g olive oil in a large heavy skillet over high heat — get it properly hot. Brown the beef aggressively in 2-3 batches, about 1.5 minutes per side. Don't crowd the pan; you want crust, not steam. Transfer browned beef to the slow cooker.
  3. Step 3: Drain the excess fat from the skillet. Drop the heat to medium-low. Add the remaining 15g oil.
  4. Step 4: Add the onion and garlic; cook 3 minutes. Add the carrot and celery; cook 4 minutes until softening at the edges. Transfer to the slow cooker.
  5. Step 5: Pour the red wine into the still-hot skillet. Bring to a vigorous simmer, scraping all the brown bits off the bottom of the pan (the fond). Simmer 2 minutes to cook off some alcohol and reduce slightly.
  6. Step 6: Pour the wine into the slow cooker. Add the beef stock, tomato paste, Worcestershire sauce, thyme, and bay leaves. Stir.
  7. Step 7: Add the mushrooms and baby potatoes on top. Push down so everything is mostly submerged in liquid.
  8. Step 8: Cook on LOW for 8 hours or HIGH for 5 hours. Beef should be fork-tender and falling apart.
  9. Step 9: Optional finishing reduction: if the sauce is thinner than you want (slow cookers vary), ladle the liquid into a saucepan and simmer 10-15 minutes to reduce. Or remove the lid for the last 30 minutes on HIGH.
  10. Step 10: Discard the bay leaves and thyme sprigs before serving.
