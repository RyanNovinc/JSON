Beef & Broccoli Stir-Fry

Description

Velveted lean beef and broccoli in a glossy brown sauce over jasmine rice — a fast one-pan stir-fry built for bulking. Makes 4 meal-prep portions.

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 4
  • Cuisine: asian
  • Primary Protein: beef
  • Calories: 860 per serving
  • Protein: 52g
  • Carbs: 98g
  • Fat: 22g

Ingredients

  • 800 g sirloin steak (Sliced thin against the grain. 200g per serving.)
  • 370 g jasmine rice (2 cups uncooked.)
  • 500 g broccoli (Florets.)
  • 28 g olive oil (For searing.)
  • 4 cloves garlic clove (Minced.)
  • 3 tsp ginger fresh (Grated.)
  • 75 ml soy sauce (15ml (1 tbsp) for velveting the beef + 60ml for the sauce.)
  • 16 g cornstarch (2 tsp for velveting + 1.5 tbsp for the sauce.)
  • 45 ml oyster sauce
  • 30 ml chinese cooking wine
  • 12 g brown sugar
  • 125 ml water
  • 5 ml sesame oil

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Velvet the beef: toss the sliced beef with 1 tbsp soy sauce and 2 tsp cornstarch, then rest while you prep everything else.
  2. Step 2: Rinse the jasmine rice until the water runs clear, then cook per packet and keep warm.
  3. Step 3: Whisk the sauce: 60ml soy, 3 tbsp oyster sauce, 2 tbsp Shaoxing wine, 1 tbsp brown sugar, 1.5 tbsp cornstarch, 125ml water, and 1 tsp sesame oil until smooth with no lumps.
  4. Step 4: Steam the broccoli in a lidded pan with a splash of water until crisp-tender, then drain.
  5. Step 5: Sear the beef in 2 tbsp oil over high heat in two batches until browned, then remove.
  6. Step 6: Stir-fry the garlic and ginger for 30 seconds, return the beef and broccoli, pour in the sauce, and toss until glossy. Divide rice across 4 containers and top.
