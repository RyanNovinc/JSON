Cottage Cheese Ice Cream

Description

Surprisingly creamy, high-protein ice cream made with cottage cheese, berries, and a touch of honey — frozen until firm and scoopable.

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 1
  • Cuisine: dessert
  • Primary Protein: dairy
  • Calories: 390 per serving
  • Protein: 29g
  • Carbs: 46g
  • Fat: 12g

Ingredients

  • 300 g cottage cheese (Full-fat cottage cheese for creamiest texture.)
  • 120 g mixed berries (Frozen or fresh — strawberries, blueberries, raspberries.)
  • 25 g honey (About 1 tablespoon for natural sweetness.)
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 0.5 tsp salt (Tiny pinch to enhance sweetness.)

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Blend cottage cheese, berries, honey, vanilla, and salt until completely smooth and creamy.
  2. Step 2: Pour into a shallow freezer-safe container and freeze for at least 5 hours until firm but scoopable.
  3. Step 3: Let sit at room temperature for 5 minutes to soften slightly before scooping.
