Palačinke (Croatian Crêpes)

Description

Thin, delicate Croatian crêpes filled with jam — 3 golden crêpes per serve, light but satisfying. A traditional European dessert.

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 5
  • Cuisine: dessert
  • Primary Protein: dairy
  • Calories: 463 per serving
  • Protein: 12g
  • Carbs: 80g
  • Fat: 10g

Ingredients

  • 150 g flour plain (About 1 cup. The base for thin, delicate crêpes.)
  • 2 count egg whole (For richness and structure.)
  • 400 ml full cream milk (Creates the signature thin texture.)
  • 20 g sugar white (Just enough for a hint of sweetness.)
  • 0.25 tsp salt (Balances the sweetness.)
  • 30 g butter salted (Melted, for the batter and pan.)
  • 100 g jam (Traditional filling — mixed berry or apricot work best.)

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Whisk the flour, eggs, milk, sugar, salt, and half the melted butter until smooth and thin.
  2. Step 2: Rest the batter 10 minutes, then heat a non-stick pan over medium heat.
  3. Step 3: Brush the pan with butter, pour a thin layer of batter, swirl to coat, and cook 1-2 minutes until golden.
  4. Step 4: Flip carefully and cook another 30 seconds, then spread with jam, fold, and serve warm.
