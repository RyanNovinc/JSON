Protein Bar

Description

A store-bought protein bar — the ultimate keep-in-your-bag grab when you need protein and have zero time.

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 1
  • Cuisine: snack
  • Primary Protein: dairy
  • Calories: 220 per serving
  • Protein: 20g
  • Carbs: 22g
  • Fat: 7g

Ingredients

  • 1 count protein bar (Any brand you like; aim for 15g+ protein and a sensible sugar level.)

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Unwrap a protein bar and eat — pick a brand with at least 15g protein.
