Greek Yogurt

Description

A pot of thick Greek yogurt — the easiest high-protein grab. Full-fat for more calories on a bulk.

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 1
  • Cuisine: snack
  • Primary Protein: dairy
  • Calories: 170 per serving
  • Protein: 17g
  • Carbs: 9g
  • Fat: 6g

Ingredients

  • 200 g greek yoghurt plain (Full-fat for more calories; high-protein/skyr style for more protein.)

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Spoon a serving of Greek yogurt into a bowl and eat — add honey or berries if you like.
