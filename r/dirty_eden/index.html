Dirty Eden

Description

Green smoothie that tastes tropical, not green — the pineapple and banana dominate

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 1
  • Cuisine: smoothie
  • Primary Protein: dairy
  • Calories: 1165 per serving
  • Protein: 57g
  • Carbs: 105g
  • Fat: 60g

Ingredients

  • 300 ml coconut water
  • 200 ml full cream milk
  • 120 g banana (Frozen, peeled.)
  • 165 g pineapple frozen (Approximately 1 cup.)
  • 60 g spinach baby (Approximately 2 large handfuls.)
  • 100 g avocado (Approximately half a medium avocado, flesh only.)
  • 200 g greek yoghurt plain full fat (Plain, not vanilla — keeps the green flavour profile clean.)
  • 30 g whey protein vanilla (1 scoop.)
  • 32 g almond butter (Approximately 2 tbsp.)
  • 21 g honey (Approximately 1 tbsp.)
  • 20 ml lemon juice (Juice of half a lemon.)
  • 6 count mint leaves

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Add the liquids (coconut water, milk, lemon juice) to the blender first.
  2. Step 2: Add the spinach and mint and pulse briefly to break them down before adding the heavier ingredients.
  3. Step 3: Add everything else — banana, pineapple, avocado, yoghurt, whey, almond butter, honey.
  4. Step 4: Blend on high for 60-90 seconds until completely smooth. Green smoothies need a bit longer to fully break down the leaves.
  5. Step 5: Pour into a tall glass. Tastes tropical, not green — the pineapple and banana dominate the flavour.
