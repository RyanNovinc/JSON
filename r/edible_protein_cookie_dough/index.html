Edible Protein Cookie Dough

Description

Soft, scoopable eggless cookie dough you eat raw — peanut butter, protein powder, and oat flour, loaded with chocolate chips. No baking, no raw eggs.

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 3
  • Cuisine: dessert
  • Primary Protein: dairy
  • Calories: 340 per serving
  • Protein: 20g
  • Carbs: 34g
  • Fat: 16g

Ingredients

  • 60 g peanut butter natural (Smooth.)
  • 40 g whey protein vanilla (Vanilla.)
  • 60 g oat flour (Safe raw; gives the doughy texture.)
  • 30 ml maple syrup
  • 40 ml full cream milk (To loosen to a dough; add a splash more if dry.)
  • 30 g chocolate chips
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 0.06 tsp salt

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Stir the peanut butter, maple syrup, milk, and vanilla together until smooth.
  2. Step 2: Mix in the protein powder, oat flour, and salt to form a soft, scoopable dough.
  3. Step 3: Fold in the chocolate chips. Eat now or chill 15-30 minutes to firm up.
