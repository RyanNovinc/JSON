Choc Muscle MAXX

Description

Post-workout powerhouse with chocolate whey, ice cream, and peanut butter — recovery in a glass

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 1
  • Cuisine: smoothie
  • Primary Protein: dairy
  • Calories: 1111 per serving
  • Protein: 77g
  • Carbs: 109g
  • Fat: 40g

Ingredients

  • 300 ml full cream milk
  • 120 g banana (Frozen, peeled. Roughly 1 medium banana.)
  • 60 g ice cream vanilla (Approximately ½ cup.)
  • 60 g whey protein chocolate (2 scoops.)
  • 32 g peanut butter natural (Approximately 2 tbsp.)
  • 5 g cocoa powder (Approximately 1 tbsp.)
  • 21 g honey (Approximately 1 tbsp.)
  • 50 g rolled oats raw

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Add the milk to the blender first so the dry ingredients have something to blend into.
  2. Step 2: Add the frozen banana, ice cream, whey, peanut butter, cocoa, honey, and oats.
  3. Step 3: Start at low speed for 5 seconds to break up the frozen banana, then increase to high speed.
  4. Step 4: Blend on high for 45-60 seconds until completely smooth and no oat texture remains.
  5. Step 5: Pour into a large glass. Best within 30 minutes of a workout — the carb-to-protein ratio is built for recovery.
