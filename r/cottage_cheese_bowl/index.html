Cottage Cheese Power Bowl

Description

High-protein savoury bowl with cottage cheese, fresh vegetables, and everything bagel seasoning

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 1
  • Cuisine: breakfast
  • Primary Protein: dairy
  • Calories: 380 per serving
  • Protein: 28g
  • Carbs: 12g
  • Fat: 24g

Ingredients

  • 250 g cottage cheese (Full-fat cottage cheese, approximately 1 cup.)
  • 100 g cherry tomatoes (Halved, approximately 6-8 cherry tomatoes.)
  • 80 g cucumber (Diced, approximately ½ medium cucumber.)
  • 14 g olive oil (Extra virgin olive oil, approximately 1 tbsp.)
  • 1 tsp everything bagel seasoning (Approximately 1 tsp for topping.)
  • 0.25 tsp salt (Pinch to taste.)
  • 0.25 tsp black pepper ground (Freshly ground, to taste.)

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Prep the vegetables by halving the cherry tomatoes and dicing the cucumber into small cubes.
  2. Step 2: Assemble the bowl by spooning cottage cheese into a serving bowl and topping with vegetables, olive oil, and seasoning.
