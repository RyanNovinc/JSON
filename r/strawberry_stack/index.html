Strawberry Stack

Description

Strawberries and banana with coconut cream — tastes like liquid strawberries and cream

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 1
  • Cuisine: smoothie
  • Primary Protein: dairy
  • Calories: 1157 per serving
  • Protein: 56g
  • Carbs: 100g
  • Fat: 62g

Ingredients

  • 250 ml full cream milk
  • 100 ml coconut cream
  • 150 g strawberries frozen (Approximately 1 cup.)
  • 120 g banana (Frozen, peeled. Roughly 1 medium banana.)
  • 200 g greek yoghurt vanilla full fat
  • 30 g whey protein vanilla (1 scoop.)
  • 32 g almond butter (Approximately 2 tbsp.)
  • 21 g honey (Approximately 1 tbsp.)

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Add all ingredients to a high-powered blender.
  2. Step 2: Start at low speed for 5 seconds to break up the frozen strawberries and banana, then increase to high speed.
  3. Step 3: Blend on high for 45-60 seconds until completely smooth.
  4. Step 4: Pour into a large glass. If too thick, add a splash of extra milk and pulse to combine. The coconut cream gives this a strawberries-and-cream finish — don't skip it.
