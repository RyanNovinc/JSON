Fudgy Protein Brownies

Description

A baked batch of rich, fudgy double-chocolate brownies made with peanut butter and chocolate protein powder. Makes 8; eat two for a bigger hit.

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 8
  • Cuisine: dessert
  • Primary Protein: dairy
  • Calories: 245 per serving
  • Protein: 13g
  • Carbs: 22g
  • Fat: 14g

Ingredients

  • 128 g peanut butter natural (Smooth, no-added-sugar. Almond butter works too (becomes tree nuts).)
  • 60 g whey protein chocolate (Chocolate for best flavour.)
  • 30 g cocoa powder
  • 2 count egg whole
  • 80 ml maple syrup (About 1/3 cup.)
  • 30 ml full cream milk (Loosen the batter if thick.)
  • 0.5 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 85 g chocolate chips (About 1/2 cup; save some for the top.)
  • 0.25 tsp salt

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Preheat the oven to 175°C (350°F) and line an 8x8 inch pan with baking paper.
  2. Step 2: Mix the peanut butter, eggs, maple syrup, milk, and vanilla until smooth.
  3. Step 3: Stir in the protein powder, cocoa, baking powder, and salt until smooth, then fold in most of the chocolate chips.
  4. Step 4: Spread into the pan, top with the remaining chips, and bake 20-25 minutes until the edges are set.
  5. Step 5: Cool completely in the pan, then cut into 8 brownies.
