Hard-Boiled Eggs

Description

A couple of hard-boiled eggs with a pinch of salt — cheap, portable whole-food protein. Boil a batch ahead and grab through the week.

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 1
  • Cuisine: snack
  • Primary Protein: eggs
  • Calories: 140 per serving
  • Protein: 12g
  • Carbs: 1g
  • Fat: 10g

Ingredients

  • 2 count egg whole
  • 0.12 tsp salt (To season when eating.)

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Lower the eggs into boiling water and cook for 9-10 minutes, then cool in cold water and peel.
  2. Step 2: Season with salt and eat. Boil a batch and keep them in the fridge for grab-and-go snacks.
