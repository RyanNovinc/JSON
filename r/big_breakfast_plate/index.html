Big Breakfast Plate

Description

The full cooked fry-up: fried eggs, bacon, sausage, mushrooms, grilled tomato, baked beans, and sourdough toast.

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 1
  • Cuisine: breakfast
  • Primary Protein: pork
  • Calories: 1130 per serving
  • Protein: 70g
  • Carbs: 78g
  • Fat: 59g

Ingredients

  • 120 g breakfast sausage (2 sausages or equivalent.)
  • 60 g bacon (3 rashers.)
  • 3 count egg whole (Fried.)
  • 130 g baked beans (About half a small can.)
  • 80 g mushroom brown (Sliced.)
  • 100 g cherry tomatoes (Halved.)
  • 100 g sourdough crusty (2 slices, toasted.)
  • 12 g butter salted (For frying and the toast.)
  • 0.25 tsp salt
  • 0.25 tsp black pepper ground

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Fry the bacon and sausage in a large pan over medium heat for 6-8 minutes until cooked, then set aside and keep warm.
  2. Step 2: In the same pan, fry the sliced mushrooms and halved tomatoes for 4-5 minutes until soft and browned.
  3. Step 3: Warm the baked beans and toast the sourdough.
  4. Step 4: Fry the eggs in the pan to your liking, then plate everything together.
