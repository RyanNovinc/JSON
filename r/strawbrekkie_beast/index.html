Strawbrekkie BEAST

Description

Mixed berry breakfast smoothie with oats — carb-heavy for post-workout recovery

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 1
  • Cuisine: smoothie
  • Primary Protein: dairy
  • Calories: 1244 per serving
  • Protein: 63g
  • Carbs: 150g
  • Fat: 47g

Ingredients

  • 300 ml full cream milk
  • 100 ml apple juice cloudy
  • 150 g strawberries frozen (Approximately 1 cup.)
  • 75 g blueberries frozen (Approximately ½ cup.)
  • 120 g banana (Frozen, peeled.)
  • 45 g rolled oats raw (Approximately ½ cup.)
  • 200 g greek yoghurt vanilla full fat
  • 30 g whey protein vanilla (1 scoop.)
  • 32 g almond butter (Approximately 2 tbsp.)
  • 21 g honey (Approximately 1 tbsp.)

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Add the milk and apple juice to the blender first.
  2. Step 2: Add the frozen berries, banana, oats, yoghurt, whey, almond butter, and honey.
  3. Step 3: Blend on high for 60 seconds until smooth and no oat texture remains.
  4. Step 4: Pour into a tall glass. The carb-heavy profile (150g) makes this an ideal post-workout option.
