Smoked Salmon Bagel

Description

A toasted bagel with cream cheese, smoked salmon, avocado, cucumber, red onion, and fried eggs — the gym-coded brunch bagel.

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 1
  • Cuisine: breakfast
  • Primary Protein: fish
  • Calories: 790 per serving
  • Protein: 44g
  • Carbs: 59g
  • Fat: 44g

Ingredients

  • 100 g smoked salmon
  • 1 count bagel
  • 40 g cream cheese
  • 2 count egg whole (Fried, yolks soft.)
  • 60 g avocado (Sliced.)
  • 40 g cucumber (Sliced.)
  • 20 g brown onion (Red onion, thinly sliced.)
  • 5 g olive oil (For frying the eggs.)
  • 0.25 tsp salt
  • 0.25 tsp black pepper ground

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Slice and toast the bagel, and fry the eggs in the olive oil to your liking — keep the yolks soft.
  2. Step 2: Spread the cream cheese over both toasted bagel halves and season (a squeeze of lemon and dill is great if you have them).
  3. Step 3: Layer the cucumber, smoked salmon, red onion, avocado, and fried eggs on the base.
  4. Step 4: Top with the other bagel half, press gently, and slice.
