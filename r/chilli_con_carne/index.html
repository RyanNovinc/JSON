Slow-Simmered Chilli Con Carne

Description

Slow-simmered beef chilli with kidney and black beans.

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 8
  • Cuisine: mexican
  • Primary Protein: beef
  • Calories: 620 per serving
  • Protein: 42g
  • Carbs: 38g
  • Fat: 32g

Ingredients

  • 30 g olive oil (Split: 15g for sautéing aromatics, 15g for browning the mince.)
  • 1000 g beef mince regular (Don't use lean — fat is doing real work here.)
  • 300 g brown onion (Approximately 2 medium onions, diced.)
  • 5 cloves garlic clove (Minced.)
  • 300 g capsicum red (Approximately 2 large, diced.)
  • 1600 g crushed tomatoes canned (Two 800g tins.)
  • 90 g tomato paste (Approximately 6 tbsp.)
  • 400 g kidney beans canned (One 420g tin, drained weight approximately 250g — the recipe scales to 400g drained-equivalent. Drain and rinse before adding.)
  • 400 g black beans canned (One 420g tin, drained weight approximately 250g. Drain and rinse before adding.)
  • 30 g beef stock cube (Three cubes, crumbled.)
  • 12 g sugar white (Approximately 3 tsp. Balances tomato acidity.)
  • 8 tsp paprika sweet
  • 10 tsp ground cumin
  • 4 tsp garlic powder
  • 4 tsp onion powder
  • 4 tsp oregano dried
  • 2.5 tsp cayenne pepper (Adjust to spice tolerance — 2 tsp for mild, 3 tsp for hot.)
  • 1.5 tsp salt
  • 250 ml water

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Heat 15ml of the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the onion, garlic, and capsicum. Cook 4-5 minutes until softening and slightly caramelised. Transfer to the slow cooker.
  2. Step 2: Add the remaining 15ml oil to the same skillet over high heat. Add the beef mince and brown in two batches if needed — break it up with a wooden spoon as it cooks. You want proper browning, not stewing. Transfer to the slow cooker.
  3. Step 3: Add to the slow cooker: crushed tomatoes, tomato paste, both drained beans, crumbled stock cubes, sugar, paprika, cumin, garlic powder, onion powder, oregano, cayenne, salt, and water. Stir well.
  4. Step 4: Cook on LOW for 6 hours or HIGH for 4 hours.
  5. Step 5: Taste and adjust — more cayenne for heat, more salt if needed, more sugar if too acidic. If the sauce is too liquid, prop the lid open on HIGH for 20 minutes to reduce.
