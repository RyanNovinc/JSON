Scramble Stack

Description

Soft-scrambled cheesy eggs piled on buttered sourdough with smashed avocado — the savoury staple.

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 1
  • Cuisine: breakfast
  • Primary Protein: eggs
  • Calories: 700 per serving
  • Protein: 42g
  • Carbs: 38g
  • Fat: 42g

Ingredients

  • 4 count egg whole
  • 40 g cheese tasty grated (Stirred through the eggs at the end.)
  • 100 g sourdough crusty (2 thick slices.)
  • 80 g avocado (Flesh only, smashed onto the toast.)
  • 12 g butter salted (For the eggs and the toast.)
  • 0.25 tsp salt
  • 0.25 tsp black pepper ground

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Crack the eggs into a bowl, season with the salt and pepper, and whisk until just combined.
  2. Step 2: Toast the sourdough, spread with half the butter, smash the avocado on top, and season.
  3. Step 3: Melt the rest of the butter in a non-stick pan over medium-low heat. Add the eggs and stir gently, pulling them in from the edges until softly set — about 2-3 minutes. Stir the cheese through in the last 30 seconds.
  4. Step 4: Pile the cheesy scramble onto the avocado toast, crack over more pepper, and eat immediately.
