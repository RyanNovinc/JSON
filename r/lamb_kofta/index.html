Lamb Kofta

Description

Spiced lamb koftas (cumin, coriander, paprika, cinnamon) grilled and served over rice with salad and a garlic-yoghurt sauce.

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 4
  • Cuisine: mediterranean
  • Primary Protein: lamb
  • Calories: 857 per serving
  • Protein: 53g
  • Carbs: 88g
  • Fat: 32g

Ingredients

  • 800 g lamb mince (Lean default; regular for juicier/fattier. Not too lean — a bit of fat keeps them juicy. 200g/serve.)
  • 200 g greek yoghurt plain (Garlic-yoghurt sauce.)
  • 5 cloves garlic clove (Mince + sauce.)
  • 150 g brown onion (Grated into the mince for moisture (red onion for the salad).)
  • 40 g breadcrumbs (Optional binder.)
  • 1 count lemon (Into the sauce.)
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 tsp ground coriander
  • 2 tsp paprika sweet
  • 0.5 tsp cinnamon ground (KEY warm note — do not omit.)
  • 20 g parsley flat leaf (Fresh parsley and/or mint, chopped into the mince.)
  • 14 g olive oil (For searing.)
  • 200 g cherry tomatoes (Salad.)
  • 200 g cucumber (Salad.)
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 0.5 tsp black pepper ground

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Mix the kofta: lamb, grated onion, garlic, herbs, spice blend (incl. cinnamon), salt, pepper (+ soaked breadcrumbs). Do not overmix.
  2. Step 2: Form into ovals/patties and chill 10 min to hold shape.
  3. Step 3: Grill or pan-sear 3-4 min per side until browned and cooked through; rest.
  4. Step 4: Make the garlic-yoghurt sauce and the salad.
  5. Step 5: Assemble per plate (rice bowl or wrap).
