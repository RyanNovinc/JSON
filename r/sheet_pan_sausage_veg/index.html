Sheet-Pan Sausage & Veg

Description

Sausages, potatoes, onion, peppers, and broccoli tossed in oil and herbs and roasted on one tray. The lowest-effort meal on the list — chop, toss, roast. Default leaner chicken/turkey sausage; pork is the richer option. Makes 4.

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 4
  • Cuisine: australian
  • Primary Protein: pork
  • Calories: 733 per serving
  • Protein: 44g
  • Carbs: 74g
  • Fat: 30g

Ingredients

  • 800 g sausage (Chicken/turkey (default, leaner) or pork (richer). ~200g per serving (~2-3 sausages).)
  • 1200 g potato baby chat (Baby/new potatoes halved; generous (300g/serve) for bulking carbs.)
  • 300 g brown onion (Chunked; red onion fine. ~2 medium onions.)
  • 400 g capsicum red (Bell peppers, chunked. ~2 large peppers.)
  • 400 g broccoli (Or green beans; added partway through.)
  • 32 g olive oil (~2.5 tbsp across the tray.)
  • 2 tsp dried oregano (Italian/mixed dried herbs.)
  • 1 tsp paprika sweet
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 0.5 tsp black pepper ground

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Heat the oven to 200°C (400°F).
  2. Step 2: Toss the halved potatoes, onion, and peppers with oil and the seasoning, and spread on the tray in a single layer (don't crowd — crowding steams instead of browns).
  3. Step 3: Add the sausages (whole or sliced) to the tray.
  4. Step 4: Roast about 20 minutes, then turn everything and add the broccoli; roast another 15-20 minutes until the potatoes are tender and the sausage is browned.
  5. Step 5: Finish with parsley (or parmesan / a fried egg if using).
