Muscle Oats

Description

Creamy oats cooked in milk with whey, banana, and peanut butter — the classic bulking porridge.

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 1
  • Cuisine: breakfast
  • Primary Protein: dairy
  • Calories: 960 per serving
  • Protein: 53g
  • Carbs: 115g
  • Fat: 32g

Ingredients

  • 80 g rolled oats raw (Approximately 3/4 cup.)
  • 250 ml full cream milk
  • 30 g whey protein vanilla (1 scoop. Stir in off the heat so it stays smooth.)
  • 120 g banana (1 medium, sliced, for topping.)
  • 32 g peanut butter natural (Approximately 2 tbsp, for topping.)
  • 21 g honey (Approximately 1 tbsp.)
  • 1 tsp cinnamon ground

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Combine the oats and milk in a small saucepan over medium heat. Stir often and simmer for 4-5 minutes until thick and creamy.
  2. Step 2: Take it off the heat and let it sit for a minute, then stir the whey through — off the heat keeps it smooth rather than grainy.
  3. Step 3: Stir in the honey and cinnamon, then tip into a bowl.
  4. Step 4: Top with the sliced banana and peanut butter (warm the peanut butter 15 seconds to drizzle, if you like).
