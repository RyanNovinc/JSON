Spaghetti Carbonara

Description

Classic Roman carbonara — spaghetti tossed with crispy cured pork and a glossy egg-and-cheese sauce emulsified with starchy pasta water, finished with lots of black pepper. No cream. Cook fresh and eat immediately; the sauce does not store or reheat.

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 1
  • Cuisine: italian
  • Primary Protein: pork
  • Calories: 1080 per serving
  • Protein: 54g
  • Carbs: 96g
  • Fat: 52g

Ingredients

  • 125 g spaghetti (Dry. Generous single portion.)
  • 80 g bacon (Streaky bacon or pancetta, cut into batons. Guanciale is the authentic upgrade.)
  • 2 count egg whole (1 whole egg + 1 extra yolk per serving for richness — treat as ~2 eggs in macros.)
  • 40 g parmesan (Finely grated. Pecorino Romano is the authentic upgrade. Some reserved for topping.)
  • 0.75 tsp black pepper ground (Freshly ground, generous.)
  • 1 tsp salt (For the pasta water.)

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Bring a pot of well-salted water to the boil and cook the spaghetti al dente. Reserve a cup of the starchy pasta water before draining.
  2. Step 2: In a cold pan brought up to medium-high, render the bacon until crisp, keeping the rendered fat.
  3. Step 3: Beat the whole egg plus extra yolk with the grated parmesan and lots of black pepper into a thick paste.
  4. Step 4: Off the heat, toss the hot drained pasta with the pork and its fat, then stir through the egg-cheese mix, loosening with splashes of pasta water until glossy and creamy. Keep the pan off direct heat so the egg emulsifies rather than scrambles.
  5. Step 5: Serve immediately, topped with extra cheese and black pepper.
