Protein Pancakes

Description

Fluffy blender pancakes of oats, whey, Greek yoghurt, and egg, topped with berries and maple.

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 1
  • Cuisine: breakfast
  • Primary Protein: dairy
  • Calories: 760 per serving
  • Protein: 52g
  • Carbs: 74g
  • Fat: 27g

Ingredients

  • 60 g rolled oats raw (Blended to a flour.)
  • 30 g whey protein vanilla (1 scoop.)
  • 2 count egg whole
  • 80 g greek yoghurt vanilla full fat (Keeps the pancakes moist and fluffy.)
  • 60 ml full cream milk (To thin the batter.)
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 8 g butter salted (For the pan.)

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Blend the oats, whey, eggs, Greek yoghurt, milk, and baking powder into a smooth batter and let it rest for 5 minutes.
  2. Step 2: Melt the butter in a non-stick pan over medium heat. Pour in about 1/4 cup of batter per pancake and cook for about 2 minutes until bubbles form on the surface.
  3. Step 3: Flip and cook another 1-2 minutes until golden and set. Repeat with the remaining batter.
