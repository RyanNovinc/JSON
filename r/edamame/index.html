Edamame

Description

Warm steamed edamame pods with a little salt — a high-protein, high-fibre plant snack you pop straight from the pod.

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 1
  • Cuisine: snack
  • Primary Protein: plant
  • Calories: 190 per serving
  • Protein: 17g
  • Carbs: 14g
  • Fat: 8g

Ingredients

  • 150 g edamame (Frozen pods.)
  • 0.25 tsp salt (Flaky, to finish.)

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Microwave the frozen edamame pods with a splash of water for 2-3 minutes until hot, then drain.
  2. Step 2: Sprinkle with salt and pop the beans straight from the pods.
