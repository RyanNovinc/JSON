Maple Muscle Toast

Description

Thick-cut brioche French toast soaked in a whey-spiked custard, stacked with yoghurt, banana, and maple.

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 1
  • Cuisine: breakfast
  • Primary Protein: dairy
  • Calories: 1050 per serving
  • Protein: 60g
  • Carbs: 120g
  • Fat: 40g

Ingredients

  • 120 g brioche loaf (3 thick slices, approximately 40g each.)
  • 2 count egg whole
  • 100 ml full cream milk
  • 30 g whey protein vanilla (1 scoop. Whisk in last so it does not clump.)
  • 100 g greek yoghurt vanilla full fat (For topping.)
  • 120 g banana (1 medium, sliced, for topping.)
  • 20 ml maple syrup (Approximately 1 tbsp.)
  • 10 g butter salted (For the pan.)
  • 0.5 tsp cinnamon ground

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Whisk the eggs, milk, whey, and cinnamon in a wide shallow bowl until completely smooth — add the whey last and whisk well so it does not clump.
  2. Step 2: Soak each brioche slice in the custard for 20-30 seconds per side — long enough to soak through, not so long it falls apart.
  3. Step 3: Melt the butter in a non-stick pan over medium heat. Cook the soaked slices for 2-3 minutes per side until golden and set.
  4. Step 4: Stack on a plate, dollop over the Greek yoghurt, fan the sliced banana on top, and drizzle with maple syrup.
