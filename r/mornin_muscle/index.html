Mornin' Muscle

Description

Double espresso chocolate smoothie — breakfast and coffee in one massive drink

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 1
  • Cuisine: smoothie
  • Primary Protein: dairy
  • Calories: 1192 per serving
  • Protein: 57g
  • Carbs: 86g
  • Fat: 70g

Ingredients

  • 300 ml full cream milk
  • 100 ml thickened cream
  • 120 g banana (Frozen, peeled.)
  • 200 g greek yoghurt vanilla full fat
  • 30 g whey protein chocolate (1 scoop.)
  • 10 g cocoa powder (Approximately 2 tbsp.)
  • 60 ml espresso shot (2 shots, cooled. Substitute: 2 tsp instant coffee dissolved in 60ml hot water then cooled.)
  • 16 g peanut butter natural (Approximately 1 tbsp.)
  • 20 ml maple syrup (Approximately 1 tbsp.)

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Brew the espresso first and let it cool while you measure the other ingredients.
  2. Step 2: Add the milk, cream, and yoghurt to the blender first.
  3. Step 3: Add the banana, whey, cocoa, cooled espresso, peanut butter, and maple syrup.
  4. Step 4: Blend on high for 45-60 seconds until completely smooth.
  5. Step 5: Pour into a tall glass. This is breakfast and lunch in one — 1200 kcal with caffeine and 57g protein.
