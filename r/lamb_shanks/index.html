Red Wine–Braised Lamb Shanks

Description

Whole lamb shank slow-braised in red wine and tomato.

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 4
  • Cuisine: australian
  • Primary Protein: lamb
  • Calories: 825 per serving
  • Protein: 54g
  • Carbs: 14g
  • Fat: 58g

Ingredients

  • 4 count lamb shank (Approximately 400g each. Ask for French-trimmed if you want the bone exposed nicely.)
  • 45 g olive oil (Split: 30g for searing the shanks, 15g for sautéing the vegetables.)
  • 150 g brown onion (Approximately 1 medium, finely diced.)
  • 3 cloves garlic clove (Minced.)
  • 130 g carrot (Approximately 1 large, finely diced (about 1 cup).)
  • 100 g celery (Approximately 2 stalks, finely diced (about 1 cup).)
  • 625 ml red wine cooking (Full-bodied — cabernet sauvignon, merlot, or shiraz. Don't use expensive wine; cleanskins are fine.)
  • 800 g crushed tomatoes canned (One 800g tin.)
  • 30 g tomato paste (Approximately 2 tbsp.)
  • 500 ml chicken stock liquid
  • 5 count thyme fresh (5 sprigs, tied together for easy removal. Substitute 2 tsp dried thyme if unavailable.)
  • 2 count bay leaves dried
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 0.5 tsp black pepper ground

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Pat the shanks dry with paper towel. Season generously with salt and pepper.
  2. Step 2: Heat 30ml of the olive oil in a large heavy skillet over high heat. Sear the shanks in two batches until well-browned all over — about 5 minutes per batch. Don't rush this; the colour is where the flavour comes from. Transfer browned shanks to the slow cooker.
  3. Step 3: Drain any excess fat from the skillet. Drop the heat to medium-low. Add the remaining 15ml oil.
  4. Step 4: Add the onion and garlic; cook 2 minutes. Add the carrot and celery; cook 5 minutes until the onion is translucent and starting to sweeten.
  5. Step 5: Pour in the red wine. Bring to a simmer, scraping all the brown bits off the bottom of the pan into the wine — this is the fond, every bit of it matters. Simmer 2-3 minutes to cook off some of the alcohol.
  6. Step 6: Add the crushed tomatoes, tomato paste, chicken stock, thyme, and bay leaves. Stir well and bring to a simmer.
  7. Step 7: Pour the entire braising liquid into the slow cooker over the shanks. The shanks should be mostly submerged — squeeze them in to fit if needed.
  8. Step 8: Cook on LOW for 8 hours. The meat should be fall-off-the-bone tender.
  9. Step 9: Carefully remove the shanks (they're delicate when done — use two spoons). Strain the sauce into a saucepan, discarding the solids for a clean sauce or leaving the carrot and celery in for a rustic finish. Simmer on the stovetop for 10-15 minutes to reduce to a thick, glossy gravy.
  10. Step 10: Return the shanks to the reduced sauce to warm through before serving.
