Turkey Meatballs & Spaghetti

Description

Lean turkey meatballs in a rich tomato sauce over spaghetti. High-protein Italian comfort meal perfect for batch prep. Store cooked meatballs and sauce separately, reheat with fresh pasta.

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 4
  • Cuisine: italian
  • Primary Protein: turkey
  • Calories: 810 per serving
  • Protein: 56g
  • Carbs: 91g
  • Fat: 25g

Ingredients

  • 600 g turkey mince (Lean ground turkey (93/7). Can substitute chicken mince. 150g per serve.)
  • 320 g spaghetti (80g dry per serve. Cook fresh for each meal.)
  • 50 g breadcrumbs (For binding meatballs. Panko preferred.)
  • 60 ml full cream milk (Soaks breadcrumbs for tender meatballs.)
  • 1 count egg whole (Binds the meatballs.)
  • 40 g parmesan grated (In meatballs + for serving.)
  • 800 g crushed tomatoes canned (Base for sauce. 1 large can.)
  • 30 g tomato paste (Concentrates tomato flavor. 2 tbsp.)
  • 150 g brown onion (Diced for sauce. 1 medium onion.)
  • 4 cloves garlic clove (Fresh garlic preferred.)
  • 28 g olive oil (For browning and sauce.)
  • 2 tsp dried oregano (Essential Italian herb.)
  • 2 tsp dried basil (Complements oregano.)
  • 1.5 tsp salt
  • 0.5 tsp black pepper ground

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Prep meatball mixture: soak breadcrumbs in milk, mix with turkey, egg, half the parmesan, salt and pepper.
  2. Step 2: Form 20-24 meatballs (about 30g each) and arrange on oiled baking sheet.
  3. Step 3: Bake meatballs at 200°C for 15-18 minutes until browned and cooked through.
  4. Step 4: Meanwhile, make sauce: sauté diced onion in olive oil until soft, add garlic for 1 minute.
  5. Step 5: Add tomato paste, cook 2 minutes, then add crushed tomatoes, oregano, basil, season and simmer 10-15 minutes.
  6. Step 6: Add baked meatballs to sauce, simmer 5 minutes. Serve over cooked spaghetti with remaining parmesan.
