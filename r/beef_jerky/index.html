Beef Jerky

Description

The most portable high-protein grab there is — a bag of beef jerky. No fridge, no prep.

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 1
  • Cuisine: snack
  • Primary Protein: beef
  • Calories: 115 per serving
  • Protein: 14g
  • Carbs: 5g
  • Fat: 3g

Ingredients

  • 40 g beef jerky (About one small bag. Lower-sodium where possible.)

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Open a bag of beef jerky and eat — choose a lower-sodium variety if you can.
