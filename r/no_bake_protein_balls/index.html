No-Bake Protein Balls

Description

Bite-sized no-bake energy balls — peanut butter, oats, protein powder, honey, and chocolate chips rolled and chilled. Make a batch, grab all week.

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 12
  • Cuisine: snack
  • Primary Protein: dairy
  • Calories: 150 per serving
  • Protein: 8g
  • Carbs: 14g
  • Fat: 7g

Ingredients

  • 130 g peanut butter natural (Smooth; the binder.)
  • 120 g rolled oats raw
  • 60 g whey protein vanilla (Vanilla or chocolate.)
  • 60 g honey (Or maple syrup.)
  • 40 g chocolate chips
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Mix the peanut butter, honey, and vanilla until smooth.
  2. Step 2: Stir in the oats, protein powder, and chocolate chips to a stiff dough — add a splash of milk if dry.
  3. Step 3: Roll into about 12 balls and chill 30 minutes to firm up.
