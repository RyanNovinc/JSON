Sheet-Pan Lemon-Garlic Salmon & Potatoes

Description

One-tray baked salmon with golden roast potatoes and broccoli, finished with a lemon-garlic butter. Bright, savoury, and calorie-dense from the salmon's natural fat plus starchy potatoes. Makes 4.

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 4
  • Cuisine: mediterranean
  • Primary Protein: fish
  • Calories: 780 per serving
  • Protein: 46g
  • Carbs: 58g
  • Fat: 38g

Ingredients

  • 800 g salmon fillet (4 fillets, ~200g each.)
  • 800 g potato baby chat (Baby/new potatoes halved, or any potato quartered.)
  • 400 g broccoli (Florets; green beans or asparagus swap in cleanly.)
  • 28 g olive oil (For the potatoes.)
  • 40 g butter salted (For the lemon-garlic butter.)
  • 4 cloves garlic clove (Minced; some with potatoes, most in the butter.)
  • 1 count lemon (Juice in the butter + wedges to finish.)
  • 10 g parsley (Chopped, into the butter.)
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 0.5 tsp black pepper ground

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Heat the oven to 200°C (400°F).
  2. Step 2: Toss the halved potatoes with olive oil, salt, pepper, and a little garlic, spread on a sheet pan, and roast 15-20 minutes for a head start.
  3. Step 3: Make the lemon-garlic butter: melt the butter with the remaining minced garlic, lemon juice, and parsley.
  4. Step 4: Push the potatoes aside, add the salmon (skin-side down if skin-on) and broccoli to the tray, spoon the lemon-garlic butter over the salmon and veg, and season.
  5. Step 5: Return to the oven 12-15 minutes until the salmon flakes easily and the potatoes are golden.
  6. Step 6: Finish with a squeeze of lemon.
