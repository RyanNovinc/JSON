Brekkie to GROW-Grow

Description

Banana, oats, peanut butter, and whey blended into a breakfast you drink

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 1
  • Cuisine: smoothie
  • Primary Protein: dairy
  • Calories: 1185 per serving
  • Protein: 64g
  • Carbs: 129g
  • Fat: 46g

Ingredients

  • 350 ml full cream milk
  • 120 g banana (Frozen, peeled. Roughly 1 medium banana.)
  • 45 g rolled oats raw (Approximately ½ cup.)
  • 200 g greek yoghurt vanilla full fat
  • 32 g peanut butter natural (Approximately 2 tbsp.)
  • 21 g honey (Approximately 1 tbsp.)
  • 20 ml maple syrup (Approximately 1 tbsp.)
  • 30 g whey protein vanilla (1 scoop.)
  • 1 tsp cinnamon ground

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Add all ingredients to a high-powered blender.
  2. Step 2: Start at low speed for 5 seconds to break up the frozen banana, then increase to high speed.
  3. Step 3: Blend on high for 45-60 seconds until completely smooth and no oat or banana chunks remain.
  4. Step 4: Pour into a large glass. If the smoothie is thicker than you prefer, add a splash of extra milk and pulse to combine.
