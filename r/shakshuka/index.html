Shakshuka

Description

Eggs poached in a spiced tomato and capsicum sauce, topped with feta and served with sourdough for scooping.

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 1
  • Cuisine: breakfast
  • Primary Protein: eggs
  • Calories: 880 per serving
  • Protein: 46g
  • Carbs: 82g
  • Fat: 41g

Ingredients

  • 4 count egg whole
  • 250 g crushed tomatoes canned
  • 100 g capsicum red (Diced.)
  • 80 g brown onion (Diced.)
  • 40 g feta (Crumbled on top.)
  • 100 g sourdough crusty (2 slices, to serve and scoop.)
  • 9 g olive oil
  • 2 cloves garlic clove (Crushed.)
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp paprika sweet
  • 0.5 tsp salt
  • 0.25 tsp black pepper ground

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Saute the diced onion, capsicum, and crushed garlic in the olive oil over medium heat for 5-7 minutes until soft.
  2. Step 2: Add the crushed tomatoes, cumin, paprika, salt, and pepper, and simmer for 8-10 minutes until thickened.
  3. Step 3: Make 4 wells in the sauce, crack an egg into each, cover, and cook 5-7 minutes until the whites are set but yolks still runny.
  4. Step 4: Crumble the feta over the top and serve straight from the pan with the sourdough.
