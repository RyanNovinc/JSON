Protein Overnight Oats

Description

Creamy no-cook oats soaked overnight with Greek yoghurt, whey, and chia, topped with banana.

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 1
  • Cuisine: breakfast
  • Primary Protein: dairy
  • Calories: 770 per serving
  • Protein: 47g
  • Carbs: 105g
  • Fat: 20g

Ingredients

  • 50 g rolled oats raw (Approximately 1/2 cup.)
  • 10 g chia seeds (Thickens the oats as they soak.)
  • 30 g whey protein vanilla (1 scoop. Pre-mix with a splash of the milk to avoid clumps.)
  • 200 ml full cream milk
  • 120 g greek yoghurt vanilla full fat
  • 21 g honey (Approximately 1 tbsp.)

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Add the oats, chia, whey, milk, Greek yoghurt, and honey to a jar or container and stir well until no dry pockets or whey clumps remain.
  2. Step 2: Seal and refrigerate for at least 4 hours, ideally overnight.
  3. Step 3: In the morning, give it a stir and loosen with a splash of milk if it has thickened too much.
