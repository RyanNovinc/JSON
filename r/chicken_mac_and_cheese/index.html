Chicken Mac and Cheese

Description

Creamy baked macaroni with diced chicken in a high-protein cheese sauce (blended cottage cheese + milk + cheddar), topped with cheese and baked golden. The comfort/mass main for hitting a surplus when appetite is the limiter. Makes 4.

Recipe Info

  • Servings: 4
  • Cuisine: australian
  • Primary Protein: chicken
  • Calories: 935 per serving
  • Protein: 80g
  • Carbs: 75g
  • Fat: 33g

Ingredients

  • 800 g chicken breast (Breast or thigh; rotisserie/pre-cooked shredded is a valid shortcut. 200g per serving.)
  • 340 g macaroni (85g dry per serve; high-protein pasta swaps in.)
  • 300 g cottage cheese (Blended into the sauce — the high-protein trick.)
  • 300 ml full cream milk (Full-cream for richness, or protein/skim.)
  • 200 g cheese tasty grated (Grated cheddar/tasty (mix with mozzarella if you like); sauce + topping.)
  • 20 g butter salted (Optional roux.)
  • 20 g flour plain (Optional roux (skip if using the blended-cottage-cheese method).)
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • 1 tsp paprika smoked (Smoked preferred.)
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 0.5 tsp black pepper ground

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Cook the macaroni to just under al dente, then drain.
  2. Step 2: Cook the chicken (sear diced, or use pre-cooked shredded) and season.
  3. Step 3: Make the cheese sauce: blend the cottage cheese with the milk, warm it, then melt in most of the grated cheese with the garlic powder, onion powder, paprika, salt, and pepper. (Or build a roux: butter + flour + milk, then cheese — the cottage-cheese route is higher-protein.)
  4. Step 4: Fold the pasta and chicken through the sauce.
  5. Step 5: Transfer to a baking dish and top with the remaining grated cheese (and breadcrumbs if using).
  6. Step 6: Bake about 20 minutes at 180-200°C until golden and bubbling.
