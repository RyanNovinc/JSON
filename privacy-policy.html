JSON.fit Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 22, 2026

1. Introduction

JSON.fit is built and operated by Ryan Novinc (a sole trader based in Australia; ABN 47 908 118 705). This Privacy Policy explains how the JSON.fit app and website handle your information.

In short: your workouts, meal plans and health data stay on your device, we collect anonymous, non-identifying analytics to improve the app, we don't show ads, and we never sell your data. By using JSON.fit, you consent to the practices described here.

2. Data Storage and Collection

JSON.fit is designed to keep your personal fitness and nutrition information local to your device and to minimise what we collect.

Stays on your device (not collected by us):

  • Fitness data: workout routines, exercise logs, progress and training statistics
  • Nutrition & health data: meal plans, macro targets, dietary restrictions, health conditions, weight tracking, grocery lists, and any budget or location info you enter for pricing
  • App preferences and settings

This information is never transmitted to our servers. It may be included in your device's own backups (iCloud or Google), which are managed by Apple or Google under their policies.

What we do collect:

  • Anonymous usage & diagnostics analytics: app and website interactions and events (such as which features are used or when a plan is imported), general device information (device type, operating system, app version), an approximate region derived from your IP address, and crash/diagnostic data. This is not linked to your identity and never includes your on-device workout or nutrition data.
  • Email address (optional): only if you subscribe to the free cookbook/newsletter on our website, so we can send it to you. You can unsubscribe at any time.

JSON.fit has no user accounts, and there are no in-app purchases, so we do not process payment information.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and maintain JSON.fit
  • Understand how the app and website are used so we can improve them
  • Diagnose and fix problems and crashes
  • Send the cookbook/newsletter if you subscribed
  • Provide support and respond to enquiries
  • Keep the service secure and prevent misuse

4. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade or rent your information. We share data only with the service providers we use to run JSON.fit, and only so they can perform their service for us:

  • Google — Google Analytics, to measure usage. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.
  • Microsoft — Microsoft Clarity, for behavioural analytics including heatmaps and session replays, with sensitive content masked by default. See the Microsoft Privacy Statement.
  • Amazon Web Services (AWS) — hosting for our own anonymous analytics and our email/newsletter.

Legal requirements:

We may disclose information if required by law, regulation, legal process or a valid governmental request.

Business transfers:

In the event of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of the transaction.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information:

  • Your on-device data is protected by your device's own security (passcode, biometrics)
  • Analytics is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) connections
  • We minimise what leaves your device

However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Privacy Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: request information about what we hold
  • Rectification: correct inaccurate information
  • Erasure: request deletion of your personal data
  • Data portability: export your workout data in standard formats (it's on your device)
  • Limit analytics: reset or limit your device's advertising identifier, or use the Google Analytics opt-out add-on on the web
  • Unsubscribe: opt out of the newsletter at any time

In practice the only personal information we hold is your email address (if you subscribed); your fitness data is on your device and you can delete it in the app at any time. To exercise these rights, contact us at support@json.fit. Depending on where you live (e.g. the EEA, UK or California), you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR, UK GDPR or CCPA/CPRA, which we will honour where they apply.

7. AI Integration and External Services

JSON.fit works on a "bring your own AI" model and integrates with AI assistants (ChatGPT, Claude, and others) only through actions you start:

  • We provide prompts for you to copy into your chosen AI service
  • You manually copy the AI-generated plan back into the app
  • We do not access or store your conversations with those AI services
  • Imported plan data is processed on your device for validation and import
  • No personal fitness data is sent to an AI service without your explicit action

Your use of any third-party AI service is governed by that provider's own terms and privacy policy.

8. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

To make analytics work, our website uses cookies and similar technologies, and the app's analytics tools use device-level identifiers. These are used only for the anonymous analytics described above, never for advertising. You can manage cookies through your browser settings, and limit mobile analytics through your device's privacy settings.

9. Children's Privacy

JSON.fit is intended for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. International Data Transfers

Our analytics and email providers (Google, Microsoft and AWS) may process data in the United States and other countries. Where data is transferred internationally, these providers maintain their own safeguards (for example, certification under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework). Your on-device data does not leave your device, so it is not transferred internationally by us.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated policy and change the "Last Updated" date, and communicate material changes through the app or website.
